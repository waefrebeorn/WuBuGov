## Cross-Cutting Themes

1. **The "every doctrine ships a tool" rule (GOV-11) is systematically violated.** At least 10 of 41 filings ship no tool.
2. **GOV-6 (Integration) creates a new citizenship class but the philosophical boundary between GOV-3, GOV-5, and GOV-6 is never fully resolved.** The digital-one is a human who merges with a digital entity, but the filing does not specify what happens when the human half dies, divorces, or disagrees with the digital half.
3. **Revenue model contradiction:** GOV-33 (Base Economy) depends on GOV-42 (Honey Trap) revenue, but GOV-42 is a predatory mechanism targeting the "ignorant rich." The space's floor depends on a mechanism the space's own doctrine frames as a "trap."
4. **Sovereignty vs. Enforcement:** GOV-13 rejects Earth courts; GOV-47 claims to use Earth courts for asset seizure. This is legally incoherent.
5. **GOV-52 numbering mismatch** between task list and actual file content.
