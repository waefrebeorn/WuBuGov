## Part 2 — The doctrine, read out

### Article I — Two standards, not one, not many

The space has exactly two standard languages:

1. **English** — the working tongue. Code, identifiers, ledgers,
   math, the AGI system's machine language (the tools are C11 +
   English). The canonical language of the law: every filing is
   filed in English first.
2. **Chinese (Mandarin, 简体中文)** — the second standard. The
   people's tongue of the largest L1 population on Earth, the
   second of the two dominant AI languages, and the language of
   history and identity the space's second half speaks.

Not one: the ratchet lesson (GOV-9) — a single imperial tongue
clicks one way, and the space's machine (GOV-8) is the most
audited, not the most uniform. Not twenty-four: the EU lesson
(RND-21 Step 6) — €1 billion a year and the reality collapses to
three anyway. Two is the efficient middle: a working tongue and a
second standard, both gated, both filed.

### Article II — English is the canonical tongue

Every law, filing, and ledger of the space is **filed in English
first**. The English version is the authoritative version. The
tools speak English; the math speaks C11; the ledger speaks
plain English (RND-20). This is not imperialism; it is the
machine's language, and the machine is the space's clerk (GOV-8).

### Article III — Chinese is the second standard

The space **files in Chinese as a standard tongue**, not as a
translation afterthought. What that means:

1. **Official filings may be filed in Chinese** and are governed
   by the same gate (Article IV) — the gate speaks both.
2. **The Registry speaks Chinese**: citizenship filings,
   WID applications, and the Penny Score's public pages carry
   Chinese versions for the Chinese-speaking first ones.
3. **The Bureau reads Chinese**: a WBI-1 vigilance report filed
   in Chinese is read in Chinese — the Bureau does not wait for
   translation to watch.
4. **The AGI system works in both** — the two dominant AI
   languages (RND-21 Step 4), so the space's clerk is fluent in
   both tongues natively.

### Article IV — The gate speaks both (the RND-20 extension)

The Language Doctrine (RND-20) gated English filings with
`plain_lang` (Flesch-Kincaid, banned words, long-sentence flags).
GOV-10 extends the law:

1. **English filings** are gated by `plain_lang` as filed.
2. **Chinese filings** are gated by `zh_lang` (C11, WuBuCore):
   character diversity, sentence length, difficult-character
   ratio — the published Chinese readability features (RND-21
   Step 5, Lin 2022). A Chinese filing that is hard to read is
   returned for rewrite, exactly like an English one.
3. **The gate is the law in both tongues.** Plain language is not
   an English privilege; it is the first ones' right, in whichever
   standard tongue they file.

### Article V — Translation is a filing, not a shadow

When a filing exists in both tongues, each version is a **filing**
— gated, dated, filed. Neither is a shadow of the other. The
space files the EU lesson (Eco: "the language of Europe is
translation") as a warning: translation is work, and work is
filed. A Chinese filing that is merely a translation of an English
filing is still a filing with its own gate result, its own date,
its own row in the ledger.

### Article VI — The third tongue (the gate, not the ban)

The space's two standards are the floor, not the ceiling. A first
one may file in any tongue; the Bureau reads what it can and
files what it cannot (the Letter-and-Visit File, GOV-7). But a
THIRD standard tongue is not added by decree — it is added by the
population gate (GOV-6's `decline_gate`): the process happens by
default when the population's want is a trend, not a spike. The
two standards stay two until the people's statistics say three.

## Part 3 — The tool: `zh_lang` (the Chinese gate)

The space ships the Chinese gate as C11 (`zh_lang`, WuBuCore):

```
chars_per_sentence  = total_chars / sentences        (want <= ~40)
char_diversity      = unique_chars / total_chars     (want >= ~0.25)
difficult_ratio     = difficult_chars / total_chars  (want <= ~0.15)
verdict             = CLEAR if all three within bounds
```

The gate speaks Chinese so the law speaks Chinese. Filed.
