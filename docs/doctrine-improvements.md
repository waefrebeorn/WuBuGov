# Doctrine Improvements — Research-Backed Refinements

**Government filing GOV-60. Research (23 sources, 7 hops) identified
improvements to the space's doctrine and practice. This filing
integrates those improvements into the existing framework.**

---

## Part 1 — The Research

Verified (23 sources, 7 hops): **Gamification** (Wikipedia) integrates
game design elements into non-game contexts. **Docebo** provides
employee onboarding with gamification. **Neobanks** use gamification
and budgeting tools for user onboarding. **Zeigarnik effect** —
incomplete tasks are remembered better than completed ones; used
in onboarding systems. **Post-scarcity** (Wikipedia) — technology
of abundance enables fulfillment of social and cultural potentialities.
**Adaptive governance** — institutions that learn and evolve.

The space files: **the doctrine must adapt, the practice must
improve, the communication must be clear.**

## Part 2 — Improvements to Doctrine

### 2.1 — Simplify the Penny Clause language
**Problem:** The Penny Clause is 350+ words. Nobody reads 350 words.
**Improvement:** Front-load the rule. "Send one physical penny per
use. That's it. The rest is whimsy." Put the rule in the first
3 lines, the elaboration after.

### 2.2 — Tier clarity for corporations
**Problem:** Corporations must calculate which tier they fall into.
**Improvement:** Add a simple flow chart:
```
Are you a legal entity? → No → Populace (1 penny)
→ Yes → Revenue >$3M/year? → Yes → Upper/MaaS (100 pennies + agreement)
→ No → Revenue >$150k/year? → Yes → Mid (100 pennies + annual report)
→ No → Lower (1 penny)
```

### 2.3 — The One-Penny Rule
**Problem:** "One penny per use" is ambiguous. What is a "use"?
**Improvement:** Define use. "One penny per project, per deployment,
per redistribution. If you use the work in 3 projects, you owe 3
pennies. If you deploy it to 100 servers, you owe 1 penny (it's one
deployment). If you redistribute it to 1000 customers, you owe 1000
pennies."

### 2.4 — The Stamped Penny Rule
**Problem:** "Stamped pennies are no longer legal tender" may
conflate users.
**Improvement:** Be explicit. "When the W Mint stamps your penny,
it becomes a token. It is no longer money. This is permanent. You
cannot un-stamp it. You cannot spend it in a store. You can only
keep it as a token, give it as a gift, or sell it to a collector."

### 2.5 — The Digital Currency Rule
**Problem:** "Digital currency is accepted" may confuse users into
thinking they can just send crypto.
**Improvement:** Be explicit. "Digital currency is accepted ONLY if
converted to plastic tokens. We do not issue digital tokens. We do
not track digital balances. You send us digital currency, we send
you plastic tokens. That is the only exchange."

## Part 3 — Improvements to Practice

### 3.1 — The Mission Board
**Problem:** Citizens don't know what to do after onboarding.
**Improvement:** The public Mission Board (GOV-51) displays available
missions. Citizens self-select. No forced tasks.

### 3.2 — The First 7 Days
**Problem:** New citizens drift away after day 1.
**Improvement:** The 7-day onboarding path (GOV-51 Part 7) provides
daily missions. Day 1: join. Day 7: choose a branch. Each day has
a purpose.

### 3.3 — The Weekly Update
**Problem:** Citizens forget to log in.
**Improvement:** The weekly update (GOV-51 Part 9) emails every
citizen with their progress, new missions, and lodge news.

### 3.4 — The Lodge System
**Problem:** Citizens need a physical place to gather.
**Improvement:** The lodge system (GOV-50) provides local chapters:
thrift stores, workshops, mutual aid societies.

### 3.5 — The Worm Game
**Problem:** Citizens need visible progress.
**Improvement:** The worm game (GOV-42) provides visible growth,
branch progression, and fulfillment metrics.

## Part 4 — Improvements to Communication

### 4.1 — The Public Ledger
**Problem:** Citizens don't know what's happening.
**Improvement:** The public ledger is the single source of truth.
Every filing, every token, every mission, every penny — public.

### 4.2 — The Weekly Update
**Problem:** Citizens need a summary.
**Improvement:** Every citizen receives a weekly email with their
progress, new missions, and lodge news.

### 4.3 — The Season Report
**Problem:** Citizens need context.
**Improvement:** Every citizen receives a quarterly season report
with their stats, trends, and recommendations.

### 4.4 — The World Congress Report
**Problem:** Citizens need to feel part of the whole.
**Improvement:** Every citizen receives a monthly World Congress
report with global stats, new lodges, and upcoming votes.
