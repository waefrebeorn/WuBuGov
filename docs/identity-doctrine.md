# The Identity Doctrine — More Than a Government, Less Than a Religion

**Government filing GOV-37. The identity issue for our
government is that we are more than a government creating
an ideology, but we are less than a religion and never
becoming one. We denounce cults. We denounce rises to power.
We denounce reinterpretations. The identity process moves
forward through the WID, the token, the ledger, and the
filing system.**

---

## Part 1 — The identity problem

A government that asks for identity faces a paradox:
- **Too little identity:** No one joins. No one cares.
  The government is abstract. The government is boring.
- **Too much identity:** It becomes a cult. A religion.
  A personality cult around the founder. This kills the
  government after the founder dies (GOV-21, the Architect
  Doctrine).

The space walks the line:
- **More than a government.** The space gives you a WID, a
  token, a ceremony, a filing system, a ledger, a ship
  seat, a vote. This is not just "pay your taxes and shut
  up." This is a full identity stack.
- **Less than a religion.** The space does not ask what you
  believe. The space does not have a cosmogony (GOV-2, the
  empty center). The space does not have a prophet. The
  space has filings.

## Part 2 — What the identity gives you

1. **WID (Wubu Identity Number):** A mod-11 checksum
   identifier that is yours forever. Not a replacement for
   your national ID — an addition to it. You keep your
   passport. You add the WID.
2. **Metal Token (W Token):** One per human. Stamped penny.
   Non-reprintable. The identity anchor. Your primary key.
3. **Plastic Tokens:** Numerous. Reprintable. Your actions,
   your agreements, your assets, your history.
4. **Ledger entry:** Every token, every filing, every earn,
   every spend — public. Transparent. Verifiable.
5. **Ship seat:** A place in the space's crew (GOV-15).
6. **Vote:** A voice in the space's decisions (GOV-17).
7. **Archive access:** The space's public record of
   everything (GOV-16).

## Part 3 — What the identity does NOT give you

1. **No theological test.** Your religion is your own.
2. **No political test.** Your Earth politics are your own.
3. **No loyalty oath.** The space does not ask you to
   choose between your Earth citizenship and your space
   citizenship. You are dual (GOV-13).
4. **No personality cult.** The founder is the architect,
   not the king. After the founder's death, the government
   operates as filed (GOV-34).
5. **No reinterpretation.** The filings stand as written.
   The AI communicator (25 years out) enforces this.
