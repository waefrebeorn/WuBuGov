# WuBuGov — Profitability & Professionalism

**The space is free for the Populace. The space is profitable by design.**

WuBuGov is not a charity and not a corporation — it is a **space economy**.
The goal: the ~ WuBu ~ space sustains itself, grows, and keeps WaefreBeorn
free to make. Profitable and professional, always.

## Revenue streams (ordered by fit with the space)

### 1. The Penny Clause (base layer, already live)
Every use of WaefreBeorn Work owes one physical penny. Over a lifetime, with
the whole space in use, this is a *real* stream — and it is the marketing
engine: a pile of pennies on the desk is a story people tell. The ledger
(`keys/coin-ledger.csv`) tracks it. **Not a tax — a gift economy with a
physical proof-of-read.**

### 2. Mega-corporation pennies (the squeeze)
§14 of the umbrella license: Mega-corporations owe **100 physical pennies per
use**. At scale, this forces real commercial conversations. The 100-penny
clause is the door-opener to Obligation B commercial agreements.

### 3. Fair MaaS terms (§4 Obligation B)
Corporations running MaaS on WaefreBeorn Work with >$20M revenue must enter a
commercial agreement. **This is the serious money line.** One agreement at
this tier funds the space for a year.

### 4. Professional services (the government arm)
WuBuGov offers the space's expertise professionally:
- **Licensing & compliance consulting** — helping companies adopt the
  umbrella license correctly (and send their pennies).
- **C11 / native engineering** — the space builds serious C11 systems
  (WuBuOS, WuBuOffice, wubuedit). Paid contracts for custom work.
- **Digital governance setup** — the same GitHub + email + Cloudflare
  stack, set up for other dual-citizen creators / micro-governments.

### 5. Sponsorless patronage (future)
Once the space has visible public goods (video editor, office suite, OS),
accept patronage in physical pennies and equivalent digital gifts. No ads,
no data selling — the space does not sell its users. Ever.

## The professional standard

1. **Everything public is documented.** READMEs, licenses, and docs in
   every repo. No orphan repos.
2. **Everything official is signed.** Seal Key (GPG) on licenses and
   government correspondence.
3. **Everything is reachable.** postmaster@waefrebeorn.org responds. The
   website loads over HTTPS. The Penny Clause address is published.
4. **No ads, no tracking, no data sales.** The space's users are the
   Populace, and the Populace is free.
5. **Pennies are counted, not spent.** The coin ledger is public. The
   space is accountable to its own proof-of-read.
