## Part 5 — Step 3: Get your WID

The WID is assigned:
- **Mod-11 checksum** (REG-2, tools/wid.py).
- **Format:** WID-XX-YY-NNNN-C.
- **Unique.** No two WIDs are the same.
- **Permanent.** The WID is yours forever. Even if you
  leave the space. Even after death. The WID is filed.
- **Bridging.** The WID bridges to any government
  global identity system (national ID, passport,
  biometric hash) but is NOT a replacement for any of
  them.
