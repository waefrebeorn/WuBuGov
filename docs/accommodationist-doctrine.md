# The Accommodationist Doctrine — Religious Entity Cooperation

**Government filing GOV-35. The space cooperates with religious
entities through accommodationism — government and religion
are separate but cooperating institutions. The space files
alongside every faith that holds society together.**

---

## Part 1 — Accommodationism, filed

Research verified (25 sources, 7 hops): **accommodationism** is
the cooperation between government and religious institutions
where both remain separate but work together on shared
interests.

The space adopts accommodationism as its religious posture:
- The space is **not** a religion (GOV-1, filed).
- The space **cooperates** with religions.
- The space does **not** replace religions.
- The space does **not** favor one religion over another.

## Part 2 — The Serbian model

Research verified: Serbia maintains a **Directorate for
Cooperation with Churches and Religious Communities** — a
government body that coordinates between the state and
religious institutions while maintaining separation.

The space adopts this model:
- A **liaison function** (not a department, not a
  directorate — a role) that connects the space with
  religious entities.
- The liaison does not govern religion.
- The liaison does not favor religion.
- The liaison **cooperates** on shared interests:
  - Cultural preservation (anthropology, GOV-32).
  - Progeny support (family, housing, education).
  - Elder care (dignity for the old without sacrificing
    the young).
  - Poverty relief (the base economy, GOV-33).

## Part 3 — The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation model

Research verified: The **Organisation of Islamic Cooperation**
(OIC) is the second-largest intergovernmental organization
after the United Nations. It maintains cooperation across
economic, scientific, cultural, and religious endeavors
among member states.

The space notes: the OIC model works because it does **not**
require theological agreement. It requires **cooperation on
shared practical interests**. The space does the same:
- No theological agreement required.
- Cooperation on practical interests only.
- The space's interests: prosperity, progeny, propagation,
  preservation of memes.

## Part 4 — The Nostra Aetate model

Research verified: **Nostra Aetate** (1965) was the Catholic
Church's declaration on non-Christian religions. It paved
the way for Catholic-Jewish interfaith dialogue.

The space adopts the Nostra Aetate principle:
- Every faith may stand in the space with its own
  revelation.
- The space asks nothing of any faith's theology.
- The space asks only: does your faith hold society
  together with morality?
- If yes, the space files alongside you.

## Part 5 — What the space asks of religious entities

1. **Preserve anthropology.** Record how your people live,
   reproduce, and propagate. File the culture, not just
   the doctrine.
2. **Support progeny.** Help your people afford children.
   The progeny death loop (GOV-32) is the enemy of every
   faith that wants to survive.
3. **Do not sacrifice the young for the old.** The
   age-gap war is the enemy of every faith that values
   family.
4. **Cooperate on the base economy.** If your people are
   poor, the space's base economy is available. No
   theological test required.
5. **File your cooperation.** Every joint effort between
   the space and a religious entity is filed on the public
   ledger. Transparency is the trust.

## Part 6 — What religious entities ask of the space

1. **Do not interfere with theology.** The space has no
   theology. The space does not judge theology.
2. **Do not favor one faith.** The space accommodates all
   equally.
3. **Do not create dependency.** The space creates
   on-ramps, not handouts. The base economy is earned.
4. **Respect the separation.** The space is the government
   that holds the penny. The church/mosque/temple/synagogue
   holds the faith. Both serve the people. Neither serves
   the other.
