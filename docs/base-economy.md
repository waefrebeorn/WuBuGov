# The Base Economy — Level One Entry for the Bottom

**Government filing GOV-33. Every game has a bottom-level item.
The space's economy creates the bottom-level entry point so that
anyone — including the homeless, the mentally unwell, the
forgotten — can earn their first coin, get their first token,
and begin climbing.**

---

## Part 1 — The insight from Roblox

In every Roblox game, there is a bottom-level item that new
players can earn. It is not much. But it is something. It is
the on-ramp. It tells the player: you belong here, you can
earn, you can climb.

In the United States economy, there is no bottom-level item.
If you are homeless, you cannot earn the bottom-level item.
There is no on-ramp. The first rung of the ladder is missing.

This is not an accident. It is a design feature of an economy
that has no floor. The space's economy has a floor. The floor
is the base economy.

## Part 2 — What the base economy is

The base economy is a set of tasks, jobs, and economic
surfaces that:
1. **Anyone can do** — no prerequisites, no credentials, no
   minimum education.
2. **Are earned, not given** — this is not UBI, not charity,
   not a handout. This is work.
3. **Connect to the extended economy** — the base economy
   feeds into the next level, and the next, and the next.
4. **Are filed on the public ledger** — every earn, every
   spend, every token mint is visible.
5. **Use the plastic token system** — base economy earnings
   are plastic tokens (reprintable, numerous, the working
   token).

## Part 3 — What the base economy is NOT

The base economy is NOT:
- **Socialism.** Socialism gives. The base economy requires
  work. You earn your first plastic token by doing a task.
  The task is simple, but it is real work.
- **A handout.** A handout creates dependency. The base
  economy creates a ladder. You climb or you don't. The space
  does not carry anyone.
- **Charity.** Charity is private and discretionary. The base
  economy is public and structural. It is the floor of the
  economy, not a gift from the rich.
- **A floor that becomes a ceiling.** The base economy is a
  starting point, not a destination. If you stay at the base
  level, you survive. If you climb, you thrive.

## Part 4 — The concessionary fund

The space maintains a **concessionary fund** that operates as
an operational ledger:
- **Purpose:** To fund the base economy's tasks.
- **Source:** Penny Clause compliance (physical pennies sent
  by citizens), mega-corporation Penny Clause squeeze (§14
  of LICENSE: 100 pennies per use for large corporations),
  and the space's own economic activity.
- **Accounting:** Public. Every penny in, every task funded,
  every token minted is on the ledger.
- **Not socialism:** The fund does not give money to people.
  The fund creates tasks that people can do to earn money. **Funding note**: Initial base economy is funded by the founder until Fast Track revenue, Penny Clause compliance, and corporate squeeze revenue exceed expenses. The break-even point is estimated at 100+ active citizens completing 5+ tasks/month.
  The fund is the enabler, not the provider.

## Part 5 — The mentally unwell

The user filed plainly: there are many mentally unwell people
who do not understand how to operate in society. The space
does not abandon them. The space does not institutionalize
them. The space:

1. **Teaches them.** The base economy's first tasks are
   simple enough for anyone to do. The task is the teacher.
2. **Tricks them into operating.** The "trick" is that the
   task is so simple, so achievable, so rewarding (a plastic
   token, a public ledger entry, a feeling of progress) that
   they do it without realizing they are operating in the
   economy.
3. **Preserves their dignity.** They are not charity cases.
   They are workers. They earned their token. The ledger says
   so. The public can see it.
4. **Connects them to the next level.** Once they have one
   token, they can do another. Once they have ten, they can
   do a harder task. Once they have a hundred, they can join
   the extended economy.
