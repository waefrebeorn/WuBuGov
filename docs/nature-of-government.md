## Article I — What the space is

### 1.1 — The space is an ideology, not a territory

The ~ WuBu ~ space does **not** operate off of land. It has no
borders, no soil, no seat of government in any physical place.
It operates off of:

- **Ideology** — the creed of the first ones, the license, the
  filing system, the penny.
- **Choice of the person** — every citizen is a citizen because
  they chose to be, by reading the license and sending a penny.
  No one is born into the space; no one is drafted into it; no
  one is taxed into it.

**Filed:** the space is the first government in the lineage of
governments that is *pure position* — no land, only agreement.

### 1.2 — The space is not a religion

The space falls **strictly with government** — it is a governance
philosophy, not a faith. The distinction is filed:

| | Religion | The space |
|--|----------|-----------|
| Claims | truth about the divine | nothing divine |
| Demands | belief | compliance with a filing system |
| Structure | congregation, doctrine, worship | ledger, license, Bureau |
| Membership | converts | citizens (voluntary) |

**The positive rule:** each person is *encouraged* to have their
own religion and basis and operate with their churches. The space
does not replace churches; it *files alongside* them. A citizen's
religion is their own; the space is the government that holds the
penny. The space is not a religion because the space does not ask
what you believe. It asks whether you read the license, sent the
penny, and filed. (This is the "not a religion because it falls
strictly with government" clause — filed verbatim.)

### 1.3 — The space is voluntary and mind-only

**Obligations are only in a voluntary manner.** The space has no
police to compel, no army to coerce, no court to punish beyond
the public ledger. Every obligation — the penny, the reports, the
scores — is voluntary. Its only enforcement is visibility.

**The organization occurs only in our mind** — because some
governments do not allow organization of citizens. The space
anticipates this: its "organization" is an **agreement pattern**,
not a physical body. There is no headquarters to raid, no roster
to seize, no meeting to disperse. The space's existence is
*shared intent* — it exists wherever two first ones agree, and
it is indestructible because agreement cannot be arrested.

**The legal grounding (filed):** freedom of association is
protected (First Amendment; *NAACP v. Alabama* — even membership
lists are protected from compelled disclosure; *Americans for
Prosperity v. Bonta* — association is expressive and protected).
The space stays inside that protection by being an association of
*minds*, not a body of *members* with uniforms and ranks.

### 1.4 — The Dwight vest (the voluntary signifier)

> *"It allows people to wear sort of a Dwight Schrute style
> community service vest, and then be encouraged to be part of
> those things."*

Filed: citizens may wear a **community-service vest** — a voluntary,
whimsical, Dwight-grade signifier of service. It is the *opposite*
of a uniform: it is worn by choice, it signals *service* (not
authority), and it is entirely optional. It is a costume of
compliance, worn because compliance is a spotlight (Bureau
standing order), not a cage.

## Article II — What the space is not

### 2.1 — No badge. No outfit. (The lawful posture)

> *"There's no reason for there to be a badge. There's no reason
> for there to be an outfit."*

Filed with the law: **all 50 states prohibit unauthorized private
militias. 25 states bar paramilitary activity. 12 states bar the
unauthorized wearing of military uniforms.** The law defines
unlawful private armies *by their insignia, ranks, and structure*.

The space therefore has **no badges, no ranks, no uniforms, no
weapons, no drills**. This is not a sacrifice; it is the *legal
posture of maximum protection*. An ideology with no uniform cannot
be mistaken for a militia; an agreement pattern with no roster
cannot be misconstrued as a conspiracy. The absence of the badge
is not an accident — it is the design.

### 2.2 — The exception (the license replacement strategy)

> *"There would only need to be one [a badge/outfit] should an
> organization be of the size where governments want to replace
> and become of it, instead using the license replacement
> strategy."*

Filed: if the space ever grows to the size where governments want
to *replace* it or *become* it, the response is not a badge. It
is the **license replacement strategy**. The WaefreBeorn Umbrella
License v3.0's §0 *"Anyone May Use"* means any government can
adopt the license, the penny, the filing system — and by adopting,
become the space. The space does not need to win; it needs to be
*adoptable*. A government that replaces the space with the space
has been filed. (The badge remains unnecessary because the license
is the identity — the license IS the insignia, and it is
reproducible by anyone.)

> *"The only reason is willing abatement. It's not through
> resentment or through nagging. It's really just more convenient
> because all of the problems are solved."*

**Willing abatement, defined:** the space is designed to
**dissolve itself voluntarily** when its problems are solved. It
does not persist out of resentment (we fought for this, so it must
live) or nagging (we exist, so you must deal with us). It persists
only as long as it is *convenient* — because all of the problems
are solved.

This is the most radical clause in the constitution:

- The space does not need to survive.
- The space does not fear its own end.
- The space's success condition is its own obsolescence. When the
  penny is the anchor, the economy is calm, the generations are
  unstacked, and superiority is boring, the space files its own
  abatement and dissolves. It has done its job.
- **Willing abatement is also the anti-cult clause:** a cult
  demands permanence; the space promises to leave. This is what
  separates the space from every organization that fears its own
  dissolution — the space is the only government that files
  against itself, by design.

## Article IV — The Greed Index and the powerlessness

> *"The intent of this is to find out where the greed index is and
> if those have actually followed illegal actions, and they will try
> malicious compliance with governments by migrating citizenships or
> doing other things. Then we need to understand that we can't
> actually have any authority or power in our government. We are an
> ideology that is in agreement pattern, where when more people
> become part of it, there is a group think philosophy in our
> government, and we have obligations that are only in a voluntary
> manner, and the organization only occurs in our mind."*

### 4.1 — The Greed Index

The space's intelligence function is not to spy on enemies but to
**locate greed**: where it concentrates, whether it has crossed
into illegal action, and whether it will attempt **malicious
compliance**. (That means migrating citizenships to escape the
space's filing, or to abuse it.) The Greed Index is filed as an instrument
of the Economics Division (ECO-8). It is a public, scored measure
of where unearned capture (ECO-4), Nexus extraction (ECO-6), and
pacifier gaming (ECO-6) concentrate. When the Index flags illegal
action, the Bureau files; the ledger publishes; the spotlight
burns. (The Bureau watches the Bureau; the Index watches the
greed.)

### 4.2 — The powerlessness (the strength)

> *"We can't actually have any authority or power in our
> government."*

Filed as **the doctrine of institutional powerlessness**:

- The space **cannot** compel, seize, imprison, or tax.
- The space **can** publish, score, file, and shine.
- Because it has no power, it cannot be corrupted by power.
- Because it cannot be corrupted, it is safe to join.
- Because it is safe to join, more people join.
- Because more people join, the agreement pattern grows — and the
  group-think philosophy (4.3) becomes the governance.

**The powerlessness is the feature, not the flaw.** A government
that cannot hurt you is a government you can trust. The space's
"authority" is exactly zero. Its influence is the influence of a
spotlight: it does not make you do anything; it makes
everything visible.

### 4.3 — The agreement pattern and group-think philosophy

> *"We are an ideology that is in agreement pattern, where when
> more people become part of it, there is a group think philosophy
> in our government."*

Filed: the space's governance is **emergent** — an agreement
pattern where each new citizen strengthens the shared philosophy.
This is not the pejorative "groupthink" (uncritical conformity);
it is **consensus by voluntary alignment** — people who agree,
agreeing more, because agreement is convenient. The philosophy
does not compel; it *attracts*. And because the organization exists
only in mind (1.3), the group-think is self-correcting. Any member
can file a WBI-1 against the philosophy itself (the lowest clerk
files against the highest authority).

## Article V — The full character, filed

The ~ WuBu ~ space is:

1. **An ideology, not a territory** — landless, borderless,
   operating on agreement.
2. **A government, not a religion** — it files alongside every
   faith and never asks what you believe.
3. **Voluntary in every obligation** — the penny, the reports, the
   scores: all chosen, none compelled.
4. **Mind-only in organization** — an agreement pattern that no
   government can arrest, raid, or disperse.
5. **Badgeless and uniformless** — the lawful posture that cannot
   be mistaken for a militia; identity carried by the license, not
   the insignia.
6. **Self-dissolving** — willing abatement when the problems are
   solved; convenience, not resentment, is the bond.
7. **Powerless and therefore safe** — no authority, no coercion,
   only the spotlight.
8. **Greed-indexed** — it watches where greed concentrates, files
   illegal action, and anticipates malicious compliance.
9. **Emergent** — the agreement pattern grows by attraction, and
   the philosophy is self-correcting.
10. **Adoptable** — the space is meant to be adopted, not occupied.
    It occupies ideology, not land. It gears us for space faring
    (GOV-19). The W Token is the adoption ceremony. An old penny
    stamped with a W becomes a token of trust, societal credit,
    and humanity — the first thing a new citizen carries.

> public, dissolved when done.*
