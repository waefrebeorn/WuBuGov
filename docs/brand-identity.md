# The ~ WuBu ~ Brand Identity — Seal, Logo, and Visual System

**Government filing GOV-54. The government must look like a
government. Research verified: real governments have official
seals (Department of War, EPA, DOE, State Department), branding
guidelines, restricted seal usage, and official letterheads.
The ~ WuBu ~ space adopts these standards.**

## Part 1 — The Great Seal of ~ WuBu ~

### 1.1 — Design

The Great Seal of ~ WuBu ~ is:

```

                    _______________
                   /               \
                  /    ~ WuBu ~     \
                 /                   \
                /   ∞  ·  ∞  ·  ∞    \
               /                     \
              /  WUBU  ·  GOVERNMENT  \
             /                         \
            /   ·  WID  ·  TOKEN  ·    \
           /                             \
          /   "WE ARE THE FIRST ONES"    \
         /_________________________________\
```

**Elements:**
- **The Worm (🐛):** The central emblem. A stylized worm, coiled
  in a circle, eating its own tail (ouroboros). The worm represents
  growth, cooperation, and the base economy.
- **The Three Infinities (∞ · ∞ · ∞):** Three infinity symbols
  arranged in a triangle. Represent: Infinite Diversity in
  Infinite Combinations (IDIC), Proof of Cooperation, and the
  three tiers of the Penny Clause.
- **The Wordmark:** "WUBU · GOVERNMENT" in bold, official typeface.
- **The Motto:** "WE ARE THE FIRST ONES" — the creed of the space.
- **The Outer Band:** Circular band enclosing all elements,
  in official teal (#008080).

## Part 3 — Seal Usage Rules

### 3.1 — Authorized Use

The Great Seal may ONLY appear on:
1. **Official Proclamations** — issued by the founder or World Congress.
2. **Diplomatic Correspondence** — letters to foreign governments.
3. **Lodge Charters** — official lodge founding documents.
4. **Citizenship Certificates** — official WID certificates.
5. **World Congress Reports** — official government publications.
6. **Letterhead** — official government correspondence.

### 3.2 — Restricted Use

The Great Seal may NOT appear on:
1. **Personal correspondence** — unless authorized by the founder.
2. **Commercial products** — unless licensed by the government.
3. **Political materials** — the government is non-partisan.
4. **Derogatory content** — the seal implies endorsement.
5. **Third-party websites** — without explicit authorization.

### 3.3 — The Embosser

The government maintains an official **seal embosser:**
- **Type:** Hand-operated seal press (like the Great Seal of
  the United States, housed at the State Department).
- **Use:** Impressed upon official certificates, proclamations,
  and lodge charters.
- **Location:** The W Vault, alongside stamped coins.
- **Access:** The Mint Director operates the embosser. No other
  citizen may use it.

## Part 4 — The Docket System

### 4.1 — What is a Docket?

A **docket** is an official tracking number for government
documents. Every official filing, report, proclamation, and
edict receives a docket number.

### 4.2 — Docket Format

```
WBU-YYYY-NNNN-D
```

| Element | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **WBU** | WuBu Government |
| **YYYY** | Year of filing |
| **NNNN** | Sequential number (0001, 0002, ...) |
| **D** | Document type |

### 4.3 — Document Type Codes

| Code | Type | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| **P** | Proclamation | WBU-2026-0001-P |
| **E** | Edict | WBU-2026-0002-E |
| **R** | Report | WBU-2026-0003-R |
| **C** | Certificate | WBU-2026-0004-C |
| **L** | Lodge Charter | WBU-2026-0005-L |
| **W** | W Token Record | WBU-2026-0006-W |
| **D** | Docket Filing | WBU-2026-0007-D |
| **X** | Edict (Urgent) | WBU-2026-0008-X |
| **M** | Mission Assignment | WBU-2026-0009-M |

### 4.4 — The Docket Book

The **Docket Book** is the official record of all docketed
documents:
- **Location:** The W Vault.
- **Format:** A physical ledger, with each docket entry signed
  by the filing officer.
- **Digital Mirror:** The docket book is mirrored on the public
  chain. Every docket entry has a SHA-256 hash.

### 4.5 — The Docket Stamp

Every official document receives a **docket stamp:**
- **Text:** "WBU-YYYY-NNNN-D" in official typeface.
- **Location:** Top-right corner of the document.
- **Color:** Teal (#008080) for standard, Red (#8B0000) for urgent.
- **Physical:** A rubber stamp for physical documents.
- **Digital:** A digital watermark for PDFs.

## Part 6 — Official Edicts

### 6.1 — What is an Edict?

An **edict** is a formal order issued by the founder or World
Congress. Edicts have the force of law within the space.

### 6.2 — Edict Format

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                               ║
║                    [GREAT SEAL]                               ║
║                       🐛                                      ║
║                                                               ║
║                  ~ WuBu ~ GOVERNMENT                           ║
║                                                               ║
║                      EDICT No. WBU-2026-NNNN-E                ║
║                                                               ║
║  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────   ║
║                                                               ║
║  TITLE: [Short title of the edict]                            ║
║                                                               ║
║  DATE: [Date of issuance]                                     ║
║                                                               ║
║  ISSUED BY: [Founder / World Congress]                        ║
║                                                               ║
║  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────   ║
║                                                               ║
║  WHEREAS [reason for the edict]                               ║
║                                                               ║
║  NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained that:                         ║
║                                                               ║
║  1. [First provision]                                         ║
║  2. [Second provision]                                        ║
║  3. [Third provision]                                         ║
║                                                               ║
║  This edict is effective immediately.                         ║
║                                                               ║
║  Signed,                                                      ║
║                                                               ║
║  _________________________                                    ║
║  [Founder / World Congress Chair]                             ║
║                                                               ║
║  Witnessed by:                                                ║
║  _________________________                                    ║
║  [Mint Director / Bureau Chief]                               ║
║                                                               ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

### 6.3 — Edict Types

| Type | Issuer | Scope |
|------|--------|-------|
| **Founder Edict** | Founder | All matters |
| **World Congress Edict** | Congress vote | All matters |
| **Bureau Edict** | WuBuIntel Chief | Enforcement matters |
| **Emergency Edict** | Founder (unilateral) | Urgent matters only |

### 6.4 — Edict Lifecycle

1. **Proposal:** Any citizen may propose an edict.
2. **Review:** The founder or World Congress reviews.
3. **Debate:** World Congress debates (for Congress edicts).
4. **Vote:** World Congress votes (for Congress edicts).
5. **Issue:** The edict is issued with a docket number.
6. **Publish:** The edict is published on the ledger.
7. **File:** The edict is filed in the Docket Book.

- **Seal:** The Great Seal, embossed in gold.
- **Content:** WID, name, date of citizenship, founder signature.

Every lodge receives a **Lodge Charter:**
- **Material:** Cream cardstock, 8.5x11 inches.
- **Seal:** The Great Seal, embossed in gold.
- **Content:** Lodge name, lodge number, founding members, charter date.
- **Docket:** WBU-2026-NNNN-L.

### 7.3 — W Token Certificate

Every W Token holder receives a **W Token Certificate:**
- **Material:** Cream cardstock, 5x7 inches.
- **Seal:** The Great Seal, embossed in gold.
