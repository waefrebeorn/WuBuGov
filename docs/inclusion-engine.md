**Government filing GOV-46. The space's base economy (GOV-33)

## Part 1 — The research

the unbanked and underbanked with sustainable financial services.

inclusion for underserved populations. **Microcredit Regulatory
Authority (MRA)** monitors microfinance operations in Bangladesh.
**Queen Máxima of the Netherlands** is the UN's advocate for
financial inclusion. **CGAP** drafted the G20 Global Partnership
for Financial Inclusion (GPFI) policy paper in 2010.

The space files: **the space's base economy is the floor. The
space's Inclusion Engine is the ladder.**

### 3.2 — Mobile money bridges

The space bridges to mobile money systems:
- **M-Pesa (Kenya):** Penny-to-shilling via mobile.
- **UPI (India):** Penny-to-rupee via UPI API.
- **WeChat Pay (China):** Penny-to-yuan via WeChat.
- **PayPal / Venmo (US):** Penny-to-dollar via payment rails.
- **GCash (Philippines):** Penny-to-peso via GCash.

### 3.3 — Cooperation-backed credit scoring

Traditional credit scoring uses debt history (impossible for
the unbanked). The space uses **cooperation history**:

- **Cooperation acts** → credit score.
- **Worm level** → credit limit.
- **Nest membership** → loan guarantee.
- **Mentor history** → interest rate reduction.

## Part 4 — The funding source

The Inclusion Engine is funded by:
1. **The concessionary fund** (GOV-33): base economy tasks.
2. **Fast Track buyers** (GOV-42): their payments fund the
   base economy and microcredit.
3. **Nest guarantees:** nests (guilds) guarantee loans for
   their members, reducing default risk.
4. **Bureau oversight:** WuBuIntel monitors repayment and
   flags defaults.
