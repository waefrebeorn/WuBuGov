## Part 2 — Physical Obligation Licensing (Research Verified)

### 2.1 — The Jacobsen Framework

Research verified (Jacobsen v. Katzer, Fed. Cir. 2008):
- **Condition**: Defines scope of license grant. Breach =
  copyright infringement (injunctive relief available).
- **Covenant**: Contractual promise. Breach = breach of contract
  only (harder to enforce).

### 2.2 — The Penny Clause as Condition

The Penny Clause is structured as a **condition**, not a
covenant:
- "Every time you use the work, you must send one penny."
- This defines the scope of the license grant.
- Breach = copyright infringement (not just breach of contract).

### 2.3 — The Enforceability Limit

Research verified (MDY v. Blizzard, 2011):
- Conditions must be directly related to the copyright
  holder's exclusive rights.
- A clause banning "cheating in a game" was a covenant, not
  a condition.
- The Penny Clause is directly related to reproduction and
  distribution (each use = reproduction).

### 2.4 — The De Minimis Problem

Research verified (legal scholarship):
- Courts may refuse to hear cases over trivial amounts.
- One penny is trivial.
- **Mitigation**: The space does not sue for one penny. The
  space uses the Penny Clause as a **engagement mechanism**,
  not a revenue mechanism. The goal is to get citizens
  involved, not to collect pennies.

### 2.5 — The Physical Artifact Advantage

Unlike Beerware ("if we meet some day") or Postcardware
("send a postcard"), the Penny Clause creates a **physical
artifact** (mailed penny) that serves as proof of compliance.
This is more enforceable than existing physical obligation
licenses.

## Part 3 — Commodity Currency Design (Research Verified)

### 3.1 — Gresham's Law

Research verified (Investopedia, Wikipedia):
- "Bad money drives out good."
- When two forms of money circulate at forced parity,
  people hoard the "good" money and spend the "bad" money.
- Result: Only "bad" money remains in circulation.

### 3.2 — The US Penny Precedent

Research verified (US Mint, 1982):
- Pre-1982 pennies: 95% copper (intrinsic value > face value)
- Post-1982 pennies: 97.5% zinc (intrinsic value < face value)
- Result: Pre-1982 pennies were hoarded (Gresham's Law in action)
- Government response: Banned melting/exporting pennies in 2007

### 3.3 — The WuBuGov Inversion

The space **inverts** Gresham's Law by:
1. **Acknowledging intrinsic value**: Copper pennies are worth
   more than zinc pennies (officially).
2. **Avoiding forced parity**: Both types circulate at
   market-determined values.
3. **Eliminating hoarding incentive**: The "good" money doesn't
   need to be hoarded because it's recognized as worth more.

### 3.4 — The Debasement Defense

Research verified (Roman denarius, Henry VIII):
- Governments debase currency to fund expenditures.
- Citizens hoard good coins, spend debased ones.
- Result: Inflation, economic crisis.

The space's defense:
- **No debasement possible**: The metal token is a physical
  penny. The space cannot "debase" a penny.
- **No inflation possible**: The plastic token is backed by
  physical pennies in the W Vault. The space cannot print
  plastic tokens without corresponding pennies.

## Part 4 — Cooperation Scoring (Research Verified)

### 4.1 — Quadratic Voting

Research verified (RadicalxChange, Gitcoin):
- Diminishing returns: √tokens prevents plutocracy.
- Vulnerability: Sybil attacks (one person, many identities).
- **WuBuGov mitigation**: Identity verification (WID) prevents
  Sybil attacks.

### 4.2 — Conviction Voting

Research verified (DAO research):
- Time-decay staking: Sustained commitment accumulates
  influence.
- Last-minute manipulation is economically costly.
- **WuBuGov adoption**: Cooperation score decays after 30 days
  of inactivity. Sustained participation is required for
  governance power.

### 4.3 — Soulbound Tokens (SBTs)

Research verified (Vitalik Buterin, academic papers):
- Non-transferable reputation credentials.
- Cannot buy influence.
- **WuBuGov adoption**: The cooperation score is bound to the
  WID. It cannot be transferred or sold.

### 4.4 — Anti-Gaming Principles

Research verified (Huang et al., 2026):
1. **Time-weight participation**: Conviction model filters
   impulsive voting.
2. **Identity verification**: Prevents Sybil linearization.
3. **Non-transferability**: Prevents reputation markets.
4. **Decay functions**: Prevent score squatting.
5. **Diversify signals**: Beyond vote counting (missions,
   mentorship, filings).
