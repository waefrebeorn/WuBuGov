## Part 1 — The Problem with Debt

### 1.1 — Debt is the Psychological Enemy of Progress

Debt creates:
- **Obligation** — the debtor owes the creditor.
- **Anxiety** — the debtor fears default.
- **Subordination** — the debtor serves the creditor.
- **Stagnation** — the debtor cannot take risks.
- **Distraction** — the debtor thinks about repayment, not
  progress.

### 1.2 — Why No Debt in Space

A space-faring civilization cannot carry debt into the void.
- On Earth, debt is secured by land (collateral).
- In space, there is no land.
- Without land, there is no collateral.
- Without collateral, there is no debt.
- Without debt, there is only trust.

The ~ WuBu ~ space operates as if it is already in space:
**no land, no collateral, no debt.**

### 1.3 — The ~ WuBu ~ Rule

**Zero debt capita per the no land allotment allowed.**

This means:
- No citizen may borrow from the space.
- No citizen may lend to the space.
- The space may not borrow from anyone.
- The space may not lend to anyone.
- The only capital is trust.

### 2.8 — The Inflation Constraint

Research (MMT) confirms: the real constraint on a sovereign
currency issuer is **inflation**, not solvency. The space
acknowledges this:
- The Coffer may not over-issue tokens (inflation risk).
- Token issuance is backed by physical pennies (reserve).
- The base economy absorbs token supply (productive capacity).
- The worm game creates demand for tokens (engagement).

If inflation rises:
- Token issuance slows.
- Fast Track prices increase.
- Mission rewards decrease.
- The Coffer buys back tokens.

If deflation rises:
- Token issuance increases.
- Fast Track prices decrease.
- Mission rewards increase.
- The Coffer sells tokens.

## Part 5 — Using Other Governments' Debt as Substitution

forward into space expansion."**

This means:
- Other governments issue debt (treasury bonds, sovereign debt).
- The space does not issue debt.

- The space holds sovereign debt as an asset.

### 5.2 — How It Works

| Step | Action |

| 2 | The Coffer converts revenue to a stable currency (USD, EUR). |

| 4 | The sovereign debt earns interest (paid by the issuing government). |
| 5 | The interest flows back to the Coffer. |
| 6 | The Coffer uses the interest to fund the base economy. |

### 5.3 — Why This Works

- **The space has no debt** — it is a net creditor.
- **Other governments have debt** — they are net debtors.
- **The space earns interest** — from the debt of others.
- **The space is self-sufficient** — it does not need to borrow.
- **The space is profitable** — it earns more than it spends.

### 5.4 — The Substitution Principle

The space substitutes:
- **Its own debt** → zero (by rule).
- **Other governments' debt** → held as assets.
- **Physical pennies** → held as reserve.
- **Plastic tokens** → held as working capital.

The space is a **net creditor** to the world. The world owes
the space. The space does not owe the world.

- The Coffer may not borrow.
- The Coffer may not issue bonds.
- The Coffer may not take loans.
- The Coffer may not run deficits.

### 6.3 — The Profitability Rule

The space is **profitable** when:
- Revenue > Expenses.
- The Coffer grows.
- The surplus is reinvested.
- The base economy is funded.
- The space expands.

The space is **not profitable** when:
- Revenue < Expenses.
- The Coffer shrinks.
- The surplus is negative.
- The base economy is cut.
- The space contracts.

### 6.4 — The Break-Even Point

The space breaks even when:
- Fast Track revenue + Penny Clause revenue + Mega-corp revenue
  = Base economy expenses + Lodge expenses + Mentor expenses.

Below this point, the space uses the Coffer. Above this point,
the Coffer grows.
