# GOV-28 — The W Token Doctrine: Proof-of-Work Societal Standing

**Government filing GOV-28. The W token is not money. It is a
proof-of-work societal standing currency: a social credit
score people earn by doing and accomplishing. People give
and receive it the way they give pennies to figures they
admire.**

---

## Part 1 — What the W token actually is

People ask what the W token is. The answer has three layers:

1. **It is not money.** It does not buy bread. It does not
   transfer value like a coin. It is not a currency in the
   economic sense — it is a *standing* in the social sense.
2. **It is a proof-of-work societal standing currency.**
   People earn it by doing things and accomplishing things.
   The work is the proof; the token is the record. Like
   proof-of-work cryptocurrency, the token says "this much
   work was done" — except the work is *societal*, not
   computational.
   A social credit score works the same way: people earn
   standing by doing and accomplishing.
3. **It is given like pennies to a celebrity.** People like to
   give pennies to figures they admire. In the space, the W
   token is that penny — a public, chained, honest token of
   "I see what you did, and it matters."

The W token is the celebrity economy made honest: popularity
that must be *earned by work*. It *records* on a public
chain and *spends* on standing, not goods.

## Part 2 — Proof of work, societal

Cryptocurrency's proof of work proves a computer burned
electricity. The W token's proof of work proves a person
burned effort. The kinds of work that mint standing:

- **Accomplishment** — building, creating, fixing, teaching.
  The thing is made; the work is visible in the world.
- **Contribution** — filing, reporting, improving the space.
  The ledger shows the contribution.
- **Recognition by others** — the penny is *given*, never
  claimed. A W token you award yourself is counterfeit; a W
  token awarded by another citizen is proof.
- **Provenance** — the token's chain records who gave it and
  why. Like a penny's mint year, the W token's provenance is
  its value.

The W token's proof of work is *third-party verified*. The
work must be visible to the giver. The giving must be visible
to the chain. No self-minting. No unearned tokens.

## Part 3 — No elections, just figures

The space does not work by elections. It works by a popular
system of figures — the way celebrities work in real life.

- **No ballot, no campaign, no term.** A figure rises because
  people give tokens, not because they win votes.
- **Popularity is earned, not promised.** A celebrity's
  popularity is a running score of public regard. A figure's
  standing in the space is a running score of *proven work
  and given tokens*. The public does the scoring. Nobody
  inherits it; everybody earns it.
- **The penny is the poll.** In real life, admiration is
  silent. In the space, admiration has a token — the penny
  you give is the vote you never had to cast.
- **Figures rise and fall by contribution, not coalition.**
  No parties, no platforms, no promises. Only the work, and
  the tokens the work earns.

This is the celebrity system *with a ledger*: the popularity
that celebrities feel as a vague public mood becomes a filed,
chained, honest score.

## Part 4 — Why this is a government, not a fan club

A fan club has popularity without accountability. The space
has popularity *with a chain*:

- **The token is public.** Every giving gets filed. No secret
  influence, no dark money — a celebrity's popularity can be
  bought; a figure's standing cannot, because every penny
  leaves a trace.
- **The work is checkable.** The accomplishment behind the
  tokens is in the ledger. Admirers give pennies for things
  that can be seen, verified, and re-filed.
- **The standing is spendable only on more standing.** Tokens
  cannot buy power over others — the space's authority is
  zero by design (GOV-1). Standing buys influence, and
  influence is a spotlight, not a sword.
- **The chain is the memory.** A celebrity's rise can be
  rewritten by PR. A figure's rise rides the chain: every
  token, every work, every reason — filed, hashed,
  tamper-evident.

The space is the celebrity system with proof of work and a
public chain. Popularity is the currency; work is the mine;
the ledger is the mint; and the people are the miners.
