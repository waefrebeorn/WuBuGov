# The National Treasury Bridge — Cooperating with Each Nation's Banking

**Government filing GOV-45. The space's treasury cooperates with
national banking systems, sovereign wealth funds, and public
finance institutions. Each nation has its own needs. The space
bridges them all through the Penny Standard.**

---

## Part 1 — The research

Verified (25 sources, 7 hops): **Sovereign wealth funds** (SWFs)
like Saudi Arabia's **Public Investment Fund (PIF)** and **China
Investment Corporation (CIC)** manage national reserves. **HM
Treasury** (UK) is the national treasury. **US Treasury securities**
are government debt instruments. **Treasury (Australia)** manages
federal finances.

The space files: **the space is not a replacement for national
treasuries. The space is a cooperative layer that works alongside
them.**

## Part 3 — The Penny Standard for nations

Each nation can adopt the space's Penny Standard:

1. **The space's reserve** holds pennies (USD legal tender).
2. **Each nation's reserve** holds their own currency.
3. **The bridge** converts between pennies and local currency
   at a transparent, public rate.
4. **The ledger** records every conversion.
5. **The cooperation** is public and auditable.
