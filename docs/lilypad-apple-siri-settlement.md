## Summary

Apple settled a class action lawsuit for **$250 million** over Apple Intelligence/Siri delays. The lawsuit covered buyers of Apple Intelligence-capable iPhones (June 2024 – March 2025) who were promised features that were subsequently delayed.

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Settlement | $250 million |
| Eligible devices | iPhone 15 Pro/Pro Max, 16/16e/16 Plus/16 Pro/16 Pro Max |
| Purchase window | June 10, 2024 – March 29, 2025 |
| Expected payout | $25/device (up to $95 if fewer claims) |
| Claim deadline | December 21, 2026 |
| Payout date | September 2027 |
| Claim site | https://www.smartphoneaisettlement.com/ |
