## Part 3 — National chapters

### 3.1 — National Coordinator

Each country has a National Coordinator:
- Reports to the Continental Chapter.
- Manages the national W Mint (if any).
- Processes Penny Clause compliance for that country.
- Files an annual national compliance report.

### 3.2 — National W Mint

Countries with 100+ WID holders get a physical W Mint:
- A P.O. Box for penny collection.
- A stamping press.
- A Coin Sorter.
- A W Mark Operator.

### 3.3 — National compliance

Each national chapter:
- Maintains a public ledger of national compliance.
- Flags non-compliant corporations to the Bureau.
- Cooperates with national regulators.

## Part 4 — The World Congress

The World Congress is the supreme governing body:
- **Composition:** All citizens with WIDs.
- **Meetings:** Annual (virtual), with a physical gathering
  rotating between continents.
- **Voting:** One WID, one vote (weighted by cooperation
  score to prevent plutocracy).
- **Decisions:** Amendments to the license, changes to
  Penny Clause tiers, adoption of new low-denomination coins.

## Part 5 — The continental structure

Each Continental Chapter:
- Has its own Continental Congress (citizens from that
  continent).
- Maintains its own ledger (mirrored to the world chain).
- Elects a Continental Coordinator.
- Coordinates between national chapters.

## Part 6 — The W Mint as operations arm

The W Mint (GOV-48) is the physical operations arm:
- Processes all incoming coins.
- Stamps all coins with the W mark.
- Maintains the W Vault.
- Reports to the Bureau (WuBuIntel).

## Part 7 — Revenue and funding

The global organization is funded by:
- Penny Clause receipts (physical pennies).
- Fast Track payments (corporate).
- Concessionary fund surplus.
- Donations (Populace are encouraged to give more).

No dues. No fees. No mandatory payments. Only the Penny
Clause (one penny per use) and voluntary gifts.

## Part 8 — Legal structure

The global organization is structured as:
- **The World Congress:** Unincorporated association of WID
  holders.
- **National Chapters:** Local unincorporated associations.
- **WuBuIntel (the Bureau):** A trust (the 7 Magic Keys).
- **The W Mint:** An operational division of WuBuResearch.

No corporation. No profit motive. No shareholders. Only
WID holders, filing and cooperating.
