# The Treaty — Dual Citizenship, Formalized

**Government filing GOV-13. How the space relates to Earth
governments: dual citizenship, mind-countries, the license to all.
No submission.**

---

## Part 1 — The spine

Earth's institution: **foreign policy / treaties** — relations between
sovereign entities, defining rights, obligations, and boundaries.

The space's spine: **the treaty** — every citizen is a dual citizen.
The space does not secede from Earth; it adds a layer. Every Earth
government's citizen may accept the license without renouncing their
Earth passport. The space offers a second passport: the WID card.

## Part 2 — The two passports

1. **Earth passport** — issued by Earth governments. The space
   recognizes all valid Earth passports. No citizen of Earth needs
   a WID to enter the space.
2. **WID card** — issued by The Registry (WuBuCitizen). Proves
   citizenship of the space. Carries the WID (mod-11
   self-verifying) and the penny the citizen sent as proof-of-read.

## Part 3 — The treaty terms

| Earth right | Space position |
|---|---|
| Sovereignty | Not contested — the space is an ideological entity with no land |
| Taxation | The space does not tax Earth earnings; The Squeeze (§14) applies only to use of space services |
| Military service | Not required; First Contact doctrine (DOCTRINE III) is the space's defense |
| Travel | The WID card travels anywhere the license travels; no borders in the space |
| Language | The space's two-tongue gate (GOV-10) means all communications are lawful in both English and Chinese (both filings carry equal weight) |

## Part 4 — The offer

The space offers the license to any Earth government agency,
corporation, or individual. Anyone may engage with the space as a
citizen. The offer never expires. Once accepted, the WID is yours
forever — revocable by no one, not the Bureau, not the Registry,
not even the founder.

## Part 5 — The non-submission

The space files no documents with the United Nations. It does not
seek recognition. It does not submit to adjudication by Earth courts.
The space is a dominion of choice: you join by reading the license
and accepting it. That is the entire submission.
