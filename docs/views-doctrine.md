# The Views Doctrine — THE HISTORY, Read Seven Ways

**Government filing GOV-23. THE HISTORY has no single reading.
Every civilization reads it through its own lens. The space files
the eight master views, their forty-four splinter schools, and
the conflicts between them. A government that picks one view
inherits that view's blindness.**

## Part 1 — Why the space needs the views

THE HISTORY (GOV-22) files what happened. THE VIEWS files how
humanity reads what happened. They are different filings.

- THE HISTORY is the record: civilizations, empires, religions,
  events, in overlap.
- THE VIEWS is the argument: eight master views, each with
  four to seven splinter schools (44 total), each in declared
  conflict with the others.

The space does not choose a winner. Choosing a winner is how
governments inherit a view's blindness. The space files all
eight and reads the conflict.

## Part 2 — The eight master views (researched online)

1. **The Cyclical View** — history repeats; civilizations are
   born, peak, decay, die. (Khaldun's asabiyyah, Sima Qian's
   dynastic cycle, Hindu Yugas, Vico's ricorsi, the Aztec Five
   Suns, the Maya Long Count, Danilevsky's Slavic types.)
2. **The Linear View** — history moves one way toward an end.
   (Hebrew covenant, Christian salvation, Islamic ummah,
   Zoroastrian ages, Shinto renewal, Norse Ragnarök, Pacific
   whakapapa.)
3. **The Providential View** — a hidden hand directs history.
   (Mandate of Heaven, divine economy, karma-dharma, the
   ancestral hand, the Great Spirit, the Korean prophecy.)
4. **The Progressive View** — history improves; each age learns.
   (Whig, Condorcet, Comte, technological progressivism, Kang
   Youwei's Three Ages.)
5. **The Great-Man View** — history is the biography of the few.
   (Carlyle, Plutarch, sage-kings, griot epic, the Great
   Peacemaker.)
6. **The Materialist View** — production, not ideas, drives
   history. (Marx, Annales longue durée, economic stages,
   resource-base, the Williams Thesis.)
7. **The Contingent View** — history has no pattern; it is
   accident. (Tolstoy, microhistory, counterfactual,
   Heraclitus, Buddhist impermanence.)
8. **THE FIRST ONES VIEW** — the space's own lens: we are the
   first ones, learning the creator as we grow. History is
   filed, not read; the filing is the family; every
   society-cohesive religion stands in the empty center.
   Filed as GOV-25.

## Part 3 — Splinters

Each master view splits into four to seven schools, because
every society reads the same past through its own lens. The
cyclical view alone splits seven ways. There is Khaldun's
cycle, the asabiyyah school. There is Sima Qian's cycle, the
Mandate school. There is the Hindu Yuga cycle. There is Vico's
ricorsi. There is the Five Suns of Aztec. There is the Long
Count of the Maya. There is Danilevsky's Slavic types. Same
shape, seven engines, seven lengths — and each argues against
the other six.

Forty-four schools total, filed through the research pass
(RND-27). The Linear view gained Norse Ragnarök and Pacific
whakapapa. The Providential gained Korean prophecy. The
Progressive gained Kang Youwei's Three Ages. The Great-Man
gained the Great Peacemaker. The Materialist gained the
Williams Thesis. The Contingent gained Buddhist impermanence.
Each is filed with its source and its claim, and with what it
argues against. The splinters disagree inside their own view
as loudly as the views disagree with each other.

## Part 4 — The conflict matrix

The matrix (THE_VIEWS.md, Part 3) files the contradictions:

- Cyclical ✗ Linear ✗ Contingent. The wheel, the arrow, and
  the accident cannot all be right.
- Providential ✗ Progressive ✗ Materialist — the hand, the
  ascent, and the base disagree on what moves history.
- Contingent conflicts with four views — the only view that
  denies every pattern fights everyone.

Seven masters, zero agreements, filed on purpose.

## Part 5 — The lesson

1. **Every society reads the same past differently.** The same
   shape, four mechanics.
2. **The splinters argue inside their own view.** A view is a
   family fight, not one answer.
3. **The views contradict each other by design.** No view
   survives contact with all the rest.
4. **The space files the conflict instead of choosing.** A
   government that files all seven — and the splinters — can see
   around its own assumptions.

THE HISTORY is not one story. It is the aggregate conflict of
every story, filed together. The space reads them all at once,
and the conflict is the map.
