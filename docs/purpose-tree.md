# The Purpose Tree — Missions, Fulfillment, and the Path of Progression

**Government filing GOV-51. Every citizen needs a purpose. The
space provides the Purpose Tree: a branching path of missions
that give meaning, progression, and fulfillment. Research
verified: the Joseph Smith progression towards Godhood model,
the Advaita Vedanta path of spiritual practice, the Chagga
Mangi (king) leadership structure, the Three Eras of Eastern
esotericism (Chaos → Equality → Transcendence). The space
adopts these models into a secular, purpose-driven progression
system.**

---

## Part 1 — The Problem

A citizen joins. They get a WID. They get a worm. They log in.
Then what?

Without purpose, the citizen leaves. Without mission, the citizen
drifts. Without fulfillment, the citizen forgets.

The space must answer: **why should I stay?**

The answer is the Purpose Tree.

Missions are assigned by:

- **Lodge assignment:** The lodge leader assigns lodge missions.

  the citizen's worm level, cooperation history, and branch
  preferences.

## Part 5 — Fulfillment

### 5.1 — The Three Fulfillments

Research in self-determination theory identifies three basic
psychological needs:

| Need | Space Implementation |
|------|----------------------|
| **Autonomy** | The citizen chooses their own missions. No forced tasks. |
| **Competence** | The citizen grows their worm, earns powers, sees progress. |
| **Relatedness** | The citizen belongs to a lodge, cooperates with others. |

### 5.2 — The Fulfillment Loop

```
Mission → Action → Reward → Growth → New Mission → Repeat
```

Each loop increases the citizen's fulfillment. Each loop
increases the citizen's worm level. Each loop increases the
citizen's cooperation score. Each loop increases the citizen's
purpose.

### 5.3 — The Fulfillment Metrics

The ledger tracks:
- **Missions completed** (by type, branch, difficulty).
- **Fulfillment score** (autonomy + competence + relatedness).
- **Branch progress** (how far the citizen has climbed each
  branch).
- **Lodge contribution** (how much the citizen has given to
  their lodge).

### 6.1 — The Three Eras

Research verified: Eastern esotericism describes Three Eras:
**Chaos → Equality → Transcendence**. The space adopts this:

| Era | Worm Level | Purpose |
|-----|------------|---------|
| **Chaos** (Egg → Hatchling) | 0-15 | Learn the system. Complete daily missions. |
| **Equality** (Crawler → Grower) | 15-100 | Choose a branch. Contribute to the collective. |
| **Transcendence** (Big Worm → Cosmic) | 100+ | Lead. Teach. Build the future. |

### 6.2 — The Mangi Path

Research verified: The Chagga states of East Africa had a
**Mangi** (king) leadership structure. The space adopts this:

| Title | Requirement | Role |
|-------|-------------|------|
| **Elder** | 50+ cooperation acts | Mentor new citizens |
| **Mangi** | 200+ cooperation acts + lodge leadership | Lead a lodge |
| **Sultan** | 500+ cooperation acts + World Congress service | Lead a continent |
| **Emperor** | 1000+ cooperation acts + founder ear | Lead the space |

Titles are earned, not appointed. Titles are public on the
ledger. Titles cannot be bought.

## Part 8 — Operations

### 8.1 — The Operations Team

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Mission Designer** | Creates and balances missions |
| **Fulfillment Auditor** | Tracks fulfillment scores |
| **Branch Curator** | Updates branch missions |
| **Lodge Liaison** | Coordinates lodge missions |
| **Bureau Liaison** | Coordinates defense missions |
| **Season Designer** | Creates seasonal missions |

### 8.2 — The Mission Review Process

1. **Design:** A citizen proposes a mission.
2. **Review:** The Mission Designer reviews for balance.
3. **Test:** The mission is tested by 3+ citizens.
4. **Approve:** The mission is added to the Mission Board.
5. **Track:** The mission's completion rate and fulfillment
   impact are tracked.
6. **Balance:** The mission is adjusted based on data.

## Part 9 — The Communication

### 9.1 — The Weekly Update

Every citizen receives a weekly update:
- **Missions completed** this week.
- **Fulfillment score** change.
- **Worm progress** (level, next level, bar).
- **Branch progress** (how far up each branch).
- **New missions** available.
- **Lodge news** (if in a lodge).

### 9.2 — The Season Report

Every citizen receives a season report:
- **Total missions completed** this season.
- **Top branch** (most completed missions).
- **Fulfillment trend** (improving, stable, declining).
- **Worm evolution** (stage progression).
- **Title earned** (if any).
- **Recommendations** (next missions to try).

### 9.3 — The World Congress Report

Every citizen receives a monthly World Congress report:
- **Total citizens** and growth rate.
- **Total missions completed** across the space.
- **Total plastic tokens** in circulation.
- **Total pennies** in the W Vault.
- **New lodges** established.
- **New branches** added.
- **Upcoming votes** (if any).
