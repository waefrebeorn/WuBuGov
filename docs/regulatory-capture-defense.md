## Part 3 — The Capture-Proof Architecture

### 3.1 — The Three Laws of Independence

1. **No single funder.** No person or organization may fund
   more than 30% of the space's operations.
2. **No revolving door.** Officers of the space may not serve
   as officers of organizations that regulate the space.
3. **No secret evaluations.** All research, audits, and
   evaluations are public.

### 3.2 — The Audit Requirement

Any organization that audits or evaluates the space must:
1. **Disclose all funding sources**
2. **Disclose all conflicts of interest**
3. **Publish all methodology**
4. **Allow public comment**
5. **Accept dissent**

### 3.3 — The Dissent Channel

The space maintains a **Dissent Channel**:
- Any citizen may file a dissenting opinion
- Dissenting opinions are filed alongside the original
- Dissenting opinions are not punished
- Dissenting opinions are rewarded (10 WT for filing)
