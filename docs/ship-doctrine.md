# The Ship — Crew Manifest

**Government filing GOV-15. The space-faring cooperative's crew
manifest: who holds which rope, which seat goes to whom, how the crew
rotates.**

---

## Part 1 — The spine

Earth's institution: **the crew manifest** — the list of who may
hold which position, the chain of responsibility.

The space's spine: **the ship** — the space is a ship. The ship has
crew seats. Seats go to proven cooperators (Penny Score, RND-1).
The manifest is the living ledger of who holds which rope.

## Part 2 — The ropes

| Rope | What it holds | Who can take it |
|---|---|---|
| The Root Rope | The Crown Keyring (GPG) | Must be >1000 Crew Score, 24+ months active |
| The Read Rope | The Archive | Must be >500 Crew Score, literacy verified |
| The Write Rope | Updates to WuBuCore / doctrines | Must be >700 Crew Score, 12+ months |
| The Watch Rope | Bureau triage (WBI-1) | Must be >500 Crew Score, no evasion |
| The Trade Rope | Penny trading & redemption | Must be >300 Crew Score, no late pennies |

## Part 3 — The seats

The Ship has 100 visible seats. Composite Score decides who sits
(Penny × 0.5 + Crew × 0.3 + Compass × 0.2, RND-1):

| Seat | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Root Seat (1) | Composite > 950 |
| Bridge Crew (9) | Composite > 850 |
| Department Heads (20) | Composite > 700 |
| Crew (40) | Composite > 500 |
| Apprentice (30) | Composite > 300 |

A citizen with a score of 0 does not sit; they stay in outreach
(the Compass Score < 300 band).

## Part 4 — Rotation

The Ratchet Doctrine (GOV-9) ensures ropes rotate. No one holds a
rope for more than 2 years without a re-verification. The Archive
audits the ship's manifest quarterly and signs it with the Seal Key.
