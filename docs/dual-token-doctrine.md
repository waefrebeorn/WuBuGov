# The Dual-Token System — Metal Identity, Plastic Asset

**Government filing GOV-30. The token system of the space:
metal tokens for human-side identity, plastic tokens for
ideas, assets, actions, and agreements. Public ledger, not
private currency. Legal under US currency acts.**

---

## Part 1 — The spine

The space has two classes of physical token, both marked with
the same **W** as the penny-based W Token:

| Class | Material | Represents | Supply | Value |
|-------|----------|------------|--------|-------|
| Metal Token | Stamped penny (copper/zinc) | Human-side identity | One per citizen, one-stamp rule, permanent, non-reprintable | Higher — the identity anchor |
| Plastic Token | Compressed recycled plastic | Ideas, assets, actions, agreements, contracts, historical moments | Printable on demand, reprintable | Lower — the working token |

Both tokens are **not currency**. Both tokens are **public
ledger entries formatted as physical assets**. Both tokens
carry serial identity. Both tokens corroborate with any
government global identity system but belong to the WuBuGov
ledger.

## Part 2 — The metal token (identity)

A human is given a metal token when they enter the system.
The metal token is the same W Token filed in GOV-19, amended
here to carry the full dual-token logic:

- **One per human.** The metal token is the identity anchor.
  It is stamped once. It is never reprinted. If lost, the
  identity survives in the ledger, but the physical token is
  gone — like losing a birth certificate. The filing cabinet
  has the record. The counter no longer has the coin.
- **Material:** a real U.S. penny, stamped with a W (GOV-19,
  RND-5, RND-6 legal framework). Copper (3.11 g) or zinc
  (2.50 g) — the same class-counted weight doctrine applies.
- **Serial identity:** WT-WID-ordinal, the same scheme as
  GOV-19. The metal token's serial is the citizen's primary
  identifier in the WuBuGov system.
- **Corroboration:** the metal token corroborates with any
  government global identity system (national ID, passport,
  driver's license, biometric hash) but is NOT a replacement
  for any of them. It is the space's own identifier that
  bridges to external identifiers through the citizen's
  choice.

The metal token answers: **who are you?**

## Part 3 — The plastic token (asset)

Everything that a person does is on their plastic tokens.
Everything they carry in their identity is on their metal
token. They corroborate and use these with any government
global identity system, but these are for the space's ledger
and identity systems.

The plastic token represents:

- **Actions and agreements.** A purchase, a contract, a
  service rendered, a promise made. Each action is tokenized
  as a plastic token bearing the action's description, serial,
  and hash.
- **Historical moments.** An event that the space wants to
  lodge permanently. The moment is filed, the filing is
  hashed, the hash is printed on a plastic token. The token
  can be held, looked at, put in a museum, kept on a counter,
  put on a shelf, or lost and found by someone else.
- **Contracts and bill standings.** A contract between two
  parties is tokenized as a plastic token. Each party holds
  a copy. The hash on the token matches the hash on the
  ledger. The token IS the contract in physical form.
- **Inheritances and transfers.** An inheritance is a set of
  plastic tokens passed from one WID to another. The metal
  token stays with the original holder (or is destroyed upon
  death per REG filing). The plastic tokens move.
- **Intellectual property.** A copyright to a song, a movie
  right, a patent filing — all lodged as plastic tokens.
  The token is the IP's physical representation. No longer
  kept in filing cabinets where old people will die and
  forget about them.
- **Society agreements.** Any agreement that binds the space
  or its citizens. The founding of a department. The launch
  of a project. The adoption of a doctrine. Each is a plastic
  token.

The plastic token answers: **what happened, what was agreed,
what is owned?**

## Part 4 — The hybrid chain (physical ↔ digital)

The token system is a **hybrid physical Blockchain → digital
Blockchain → physical coin**:

1. **Physical token created.** A metal or plastic token is
   marked with its serial, description, and a SHA-256 hash
   linking it to the previous token in its chain (the same
   PennyChain pattern from RND-3).
2. **Digital ledger records.** The token's serial,
   description, hash, and current holder are filed on the
   public PennyChain ledger (keyless, KSI-pattern, RND-3).
3. **Physical coin circulates.** The token can be held,
   traded, lost, found, displayed, or destroyed. The physical
   asset is **much more important than currency or token**.
   The currency and token itself is carried, but the action
   or contract or agreement or physical asset is what the
   token represents.
4. **Digital verification.** Anyone with the token can verify
   its hash against the public ledger. The hash proves the
   token's existence and ownership at the time of minting.
   The token's physical condition does not affect the ledger.

This hybrid system means: **the physical asset is primary,
the digital record is proof, the token is the bridge.**

## Part 5 — The intentional confusion

The confusion of the coin holder is an **intentional
whimsical reintroduction of the physical asset**.

When someone finds a plastic token on the street, they do not
immediately know what it represents. They see a W-marked
plastic coin with a serial and a hash. They look it up on
the public ledger. They discover: this token is a contract
between Citizen A and Citizen B, dated 2026-09-09, for the
provision of AI training services. Or: this token is the
filing of GOV-30. Or: this token is an inheritance of 50
pennies from Citizen C to Citizen D.

The finder's confusion is intentional. It reintroduces the
physical asset as something **mysterious, holdable, and
worth investigating**. In a digital world, the physical token
is a curiosity. In a physical world, the plastic token is a
receipt. In the space's world, it is **both and neither**.

## Part 6 — The reprintability hierarchy

| Token | Material | Reprintable? | Why |
|-------|----------|--------------|-----|
| Metal Token | Stamped penny | **No** | One per human. Identity is singular. Losing the physical token is like losing a birth certificate — the ledger survives, the physical is gone. |
| Plastic Token | Compressed plastic | **Yes** | Ideas, assets, actions, and agreements are numerous. Plastic is reprintable. If a token is lost or damaged, the ledger entry survives and a new token can be printed with the same serial and hash. |

This creates a value hierarchy that mirrors the real world:
**identity is rare and permanent, ideas and actions are
plentiful and reprintable.**

## Part 7 — Legal compliance (US currency acts)

The token system is a **public ledger system, not a private
currency**. It maintains compliance with US currency acts as
filed in RND-5:

- **No token is intended as current money.** The tokens are
  **claims on assets, actions, and agreements filed on the
  public ledger**. They do not circulate as money. They
  circulate as **proof of filing**.
- **The metal token** is a real U.S. penny, stamped with a W
  (GOV-19, RND-5 legal framework). The stamping is legal
  under 31 C.F.R. § 82.2(b) (novelty/art treatment of coins
  is expressly permitted). The penny remains legal tender in
  perpetuity (Treasury FAQ).
- **The plastic token** is compressed recycled plastic,
  marked with a W. It is not a coin. It is not made of metal.
  It does not resemble US currency. It is a **filing artifact**
  — the same legal category as a commemorative token, a
  loyalty chip, or a casino token.
- **The public ledger** (PennyChain) records all tokens.
  No new money is created. The ledger records **proof of
  existence and ownership** of the filed asset or agreement.
- **No token competes with Federal currency.** No token is
  denominated in dollars. No token is used to pay wages or
  debts denominated in dollars. The tokens are the space's
  internal filing system, made physical.

Each purchase or transaction is always made as an asset
similar to how digital storefronts have been trying to do so
— but instead actually doing so with a coin. The express
purpose is that this is **not funny money and is not intended
to be a currency replacement**. The express purpose is a
**public ledger format where each purchase is always made as
an asset** — a physical representation of a filing.

## Part 8 — The corroboration system

When a person introduces themselves into the system:

1. They receive a **metal token** (their identity anchor).
2. They receive a set of **plastic tokens** (their initial
   filing set: citizenship, start balance, Ship seat, first
   action).
3. They use the **metal token to validate** with their
   **plastic tokens** — the metal token's serial is the
   primary key that links all plastic tokens to the citizen.
4. They use these with any government global identity system
   through choice — the space does not force replacement of
   external identifiers, it bridges to them.

Everything that a person does is on their plastic tokens.
Everything that they carry in their identity is on their
metal token. They could corroborate and use these with any
government global identity system, but these are for the
space's ledger and identity systems.
