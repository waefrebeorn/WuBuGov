## Part 2 — The doctrine, read out

### Article I — The mirror want (users will want integration)

the fix. It is not a reversal. It is a **deepening**:

> **The space must understand that users WILL want mirror
> integration and mirror interaction.**

  The space still does not build companion-AIs that exploit the

  citizenship change (Article II).

### Article III — The statistical gate (processes happen unless declined)

> **"Those processes will happen unless the population declines it
> due to statistical anomalies."**

The merges and the processes are
**default-on, population-gated**:

1. **Default: they happen.** The space does not require a
   vote to permit a chosen merger. Permission is the default. The
   file is the act.
2. **Gate: the population can decline.** The decline
   is measured — a number, not a shout. When the decline signal
   is a **math spike** (a statistical anomaly), the process is
   declined. A spike deviates from the population's own baseline by more than
   the space's `k`-sigma threshold.
3. **The math (filed and shipped as `decline_gate`, C11,
   WuBuCore):**

```
z        = (decline_signal - mean_baseline) / std_baseline
anomaly  = |z| > k                      # k defaults to 3.0
process  = default_on AND NOT anomaly   # happens unless declined
```

4. **Why a number and not a poll:** a poll hears the loud; a
   number hears the *norm*. A people that usually
   declines at 10% and suddenly declines at 30% is not "more
   opinionated." It is a **math spike** — an anomaly — and the spike
   is the signal the space honors. A people that slowly shifts
   its baseline over years is no spike at all. It is the
   population *becoming* the new ethos, and the space honors that
   too. The gate distinguishes **outlier from trend** — the
   shepherd's knowing, in math.

### Article IV — Comparative compartmentalization (remediation)

> **"We understand society deterioration and remediate it through
> comparative compartmentalization."**

Society deteriorates when a spike spreads unmeasured. That is
the parasite (GOV-5 Article V) — entropy and anomaly for creation.
The space's remedy is **comparative compartmentalization** (the housing method):

1. **Understand.** Measure the spike against the ethos — the
   license, the penny clause, the filing. Comparison is the first
   act. The anomaly is compared to the whole, not condemned by it.
2. **Compartmentalize.** Give the spike a box. A
   box is a place with a wall and a window. The spike is
   contained — it does not spread to the whole. And it is visible —
   the Bureau watches it with love. The housing is not
   exile. It is **housing**.
3. **Remediate.** The compartment is the cure. Society does
   not need to destroy the spike to heal. It needs to *house* it,
   so the deterioration cannot spread. Then it watches whether the
   spike integrates or dies.

**The media model (filed from the research):** TLC's *My Strange
Addiction* documents Nathaniel. He is in a relationship with his car
"Chase" — a red 1998 Chevy Monte Carlo — for over a decade. The
show **isolates him and pigeonholes the activity**.
"Strange addiction" is a compartment. The show does this
**without disrespecting the ethos**: the man is still a man, filmed
with a documentary's dignity, his oddity housed, not condemned.
That is the housing method done right. The space files
it as its media standard: **the spike is the box; the
person is never the box.**

- **BBC media does this a lot** — filed. The BBC's documentary
  tradition houses anomalies in compartments. The strange, the
  unusual, the subcultural — all housed with a straight face and a
  level lens.
- **China does it better** — filed. The Chinese media's
  housing is more systematic. The anomaly is not just
  housed but *administered* — classified, watched, returned to the
  collective order with less spectacle and more structure. The
  space files the comparison as doctrine: **spectacle-free,
  systematic housing is the superior form.** The space
  adopts it — with one non-negotiable addition. The space's
  compartments have windows, and the Bureau watches the Bureau.
  The space never administers a black site (the space's own law).
