# The Forces Doctrine — The Mashy Mishy Timeline

**Government filing GOV-24. Seven forces drive the timeline:
Wrath, War, Greed, Identity, Classism, Wealth Dynamic, and
Agism. They are not clean tracks; each feeds the others, all
tangled at once. The mashy mishy is the point — history is
one braid, not seven lines.**

---

## Part 1 — The seven forces

1. **Wrath** — injustice felt as fire. Engine: injury that
   finally ignites. (Ionian Revolt, Peasants' Revolt, the
   Bastille, Haiti, the Commune, the Arab Spring.)
2. **War** — organized violence as policy. Engine: states
   define themselves at the line. (Bronze Age collapse, the
   Mongols, the World Wars.)
3. **Greed** — capture without contribution. Engine: the hoard
   that outgrows the holder. (Roman latifundia, the East India
   Company, 2008.) The space measures this one — the Greed
   Index (ECO-8).
4. **Identity** — the line between us and them. Engine: who
   counts, drawn and redrawn. (Persian Wars, diaspora, the
   Crusades, the nation, identity politics.)
5. **Classism** — rank enforced as nature. Engine: someone is
   always below. (Caste, patricians and plebeians, serfdom,
   the estates, plantations, the proletariat.)
6. **Wealth Dynamic** — value moving, or refusing to move.
   Engine: flows, dams, floods, dry beds. (Cowries, the Lydian
   coin, the Silk Roads, the Atlantic system, the penny, the
   W Token.)
7. **Agism** — age as rank and rhythm. Engine: elders hold,
   youth storm, the cycle turns. (Sparta's elders, Khaldun's
   three generations, the 1960s, the generation war.) The
   Ratchet (GOV-9) and the Architect Doctrine (GOV-21) are the
   space's agism filings.

## Part 2 — The mashy mishy

The forces do not take turns. Every era is several forces at
once, feeding each other:

- Bronze Age collapse: war + identity + wealth dynamic.
- Roman decline: greed + classism + wrath + war.
- The Crusades: identity + war + greed — the cross and the
  coin.
- The Atlantic world: greed + classism + wealth dynamic +
  identity.
- The present: agism + identity + wealth dynamic + wrath +
  greed.

## Part 3 — The feedback loop

The forces loop. Greed captures; capture becomes class; class
feels like wrath; wrath starts war; war moves wealth; wealth
re-feeds greed. Identity picks the sides. Agism turns the
generations beneath it all. Pull one thread and the whole braid
moves.

## Part 4 — Why file the mash

1. **No force acts alone.** The French Revolution was wrath.
   It was also classism, wealth dynamic, and identity arriving
   together. Pick one cause and you have a tract, not a
   history.
2. **The same braid runs through the space.** The space's
   instruments sit on the loop. The Greed Index measures greed.
   The W Token moves wealth. The Ratchet rotates the old. The
   Vote gives wrath a filing instead of a fire. The space does
   not pretend the forces are gone. It files them.
3. **The mash is not chaos; it is the mechanism.** A timeline
   with clean tracks is a lie a civilization tells itself. The
   mashy mishy is the truth. History is one braid, and the
   braid is what the space reads.
