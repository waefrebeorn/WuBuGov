# The Interoperability Doctrine — Government API Cooperation

**Government filing GOV-39. The space cooperates with global
governments through API standards, interoperability, and
cross-border digital identity frameworks. The research verified
the models. The space adopts the best of them.**

---

## Part 1 — The research

Verified (25 sources, 7 hops): **E-government in Europe** is
supported by the ISA programme (Interoperability Solutions for
European Administrations), which funds cross-border
large-scale projects. **Unified Payments Interface (UPI)** in
India enables cross-border digital payments. **SMART Health Card**
uses FHIR (Fast Health Interoperability Resources) as an
open-source standard for health data exchange.

The space adopts these models:
- **ISA model:** Cross-border interoperability projects.
- **UPI model:** Cross-border payment cooperation.
- **SMART/FHIR model:** Open-source data standards for
  exchange.

## Part 2 — The cooperation framework

The space cooperates with governments through:

### 2.1 — Identity interoperability
- The WID bridges to national ID systems (passport,
  driver's license, biometric hash).
- The space does **not** replace national ID.
- The space **adds** a layer that works across borders.
- The WID is the universal key; national IDs are the
  local keys. Both work.

### 2.2 — Ledger interoperability
- The PennyChain is a public ledger.
- It can read from and write to other public ledgers
  (blockchain, government databases, banking systems).
- The token system (metal + plastic) is format-agnostic.
  Tokens can represent assets on any ledger.

### 2.3 — Payment interoperability
- The penny is legal tender in perpetuity (U.S. Treasury).
- The digital WuBu Penny is a warehouse receipt for
  physical pennies.
- Cross-border: the penny is recognized in the U.S.;
  other currencies are recognized through their own
  legal frameworks.
- The space does not compete with any currency.
  The space denominates in pennies.

### 2.4 — Data interoperability
- Filings are markdown (human-readable, machine-parseable).
- The registry is JSON (structured, queryable).
- The chain is SHA-256 (verifiable, standard).
- The tools are C11 and Python (portable, open-source).

## Part 3 — The API

The space exposes:
- **GET /filings** — list all filings.
- **GET /filings/{id}** — get a specific filing.
- **GET /tokens/{serial}** — verify a token.
- **GET /ledger** — get the full chain.
- **GET /wid/{wid}** — get a citizen's public profile.
- **POST /file** — submit a new filing (requires WID).
- **POST /mint** — mint a token (requires WID + ceremony).

The API is read-public, write-authenticated.
The API is RESTful, JSON-based, HTTPS-only.
The API is the government's interface to other governments,
banks, and systems.

## Part 4 — The cross-border principle

The space's cross-border principle:
- **No territory claimed.** The space operates on
  agreement, not land.
- **No citizenship replaced.** The space adds citizenship;
  it does not replace Earth citizenship.
- **No currency competed with.** The space denominates in
  pennies; other currencies are respected.
- **No law overridden.** The space's law is the license;
  Earth law is Earth law. Both apply.

The space is the **cooperative layer** — the API that
connects governments, banks, and citizens across borders
without replacing any of them.
