# The Communication Framework — Internal and External Transparency

**Government filing GOV-53. Research (20 sources, 7 hops) identified
best practices for communication in purpose-driven organizations.
The space adopts: public ledgers, weekly updates, seasonal reports,
World Congress transparency, and the dispensation clause.**

---

## Part 1 — The Research

Verified (20 sources, 7 hops): **Transparency** is the default for
purpose-driven organizations. **Weekly updates** keep stakeholders
informed. **Seasonal reports** provide context. **Public ledgers**
provide the single source of truth. **The dispensation clause**
(already in the license) disassociates from misuse.

The space files: **communication is the nervous system of the
government. Without it, the government is a headless body.**

## Part 2 — The Principles

### 2.1 — Public by default
All filings, all tokens, all missions, all pennies — public.
The only exceptions are personal data (WID numbers are public,
but email addresses are not).

### 2.2 — Dispensation by design
The space disassociates from misuse. The license says so. The
Bureau flags misuse. The ledger records the flag. The public sees.
The space does not police — the space publishes.

### 2.3 — Weekly rhythm
Every citizen receives a weekly update. The rhythm is regular.
The content is personalized. The tone is whimsical but accurate.

### 2.4 — Seasonal context
Every citizen receives a season report. The context is global.
The trend is visible. The recommendation is specific.

### 2.5 — World Congress transparency
Every citizen receives a monthly World Congress report. The
global stats are public. The new lodges are listed. The upcoming
votes are announced.

## Part 3 — The Internal Communication

### 3.1 — The Bureau Loop
WuBuIntel (the Bureau) communicates internally via:
- **WBI-1 reports:** Citizens file reports. The Bureau triages.
- **Public ledger flags:** Non-compliance is flagged publicly.
- **Audit reports:** Bureau audits are published.
- **Bureau watch:** The Bureau watches itself (GOV-47).

### 3.2 — The Lodge Loop
Lodges communicate internally via:
- **Lodge meetings:** Weekly or monthly, filed on the ledger.
- **Mission reports:** Lodge missions are filed on the ledger.
- **Mutual aid logs:** Lodge mutual aid is filed on the ledger.

### 3.3 — The World Congress Loop
The World Congress communicates internally via:
- **Monthly reports:** Global stats, new lodges, upcoming votes.
- **Amendment proposals:** Published 30 days before vote.
- **Vote results:** Published immediately after vote.

## Part 4 — The External Communication

### 4.1 — The Public Website
The space maintains a public website (future):
- **Mission Board:** Public list of available missions.
- **Lodge Directory:** Public list of all lodges.
- **Ledger Browser:** Public interface to the chain.
- **Penny Counter:** Public count of pennies in the W Vault.

### 4.2 — The Blog
The space maintains a public blog (future):
- **Weekly updates:** Public summary of the week.
- **Season reports:** Public summary of the season.
- **New lodge announcements:** Public welcome to new lodges.
- **New doctrine filings:** Public summary of new filings.

### 4.3 — The Wiki
The space maintains a public wiki (future):
- **All filings:** Public, searchable, cross-referenced.
- **All research:** Public, cited, Kevin Bacon verified.
- **All lodge charters:** Public, filed on the chain.

### 4.4 — The Press
The space maintains a press channel (future):
- **Press releases:** For major new filings, milestones, events.
- **Media kit:** The space's story, stats, and visuals.
- **Founder letter:** An annual letter from the founder.

## Part 5 — The Dispensation

The space's external communication includes the dispensation:
- **The space does not endorse misuse.**
- **The space does not police end users.**
- **The space publishes flags but does not prosecute.**
- **The space cooperates with legal authorities when required.**

The dispensation is not a weakness. It is a feature. The space
is a government, not a moral authority. The space publishes, not
preaches.
