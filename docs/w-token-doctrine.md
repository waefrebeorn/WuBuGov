# The W Token — The Adoption Ceremony

**Government filing GOV-19. The space is an adoptable government.
It does not occupy land. It occupies ideology. It gears us for
space faring. The old penny is the old penny. The W Token is the
new trust.**

---

## Part 1 — The spine

Earth's institution: **the oath** — a public moment that turns a
person into a citizen, witnessed by others, recorded forever.

The space's spine: **the W Token** — a ceremony that turns an old
penny into a token of trust. A machine stamps a W on the penny.
The penny stops being money. It becomes a token of humanity. It
becomes societal credit the person owns for life.

## Part 2 — The adoptable government

The space does not take land. It has no border, no flag on a map,
no army. It offers an idea: how to live, how to earn, how to
trust, how to leave the ground.

A person joins by choice. They read the doctrine. They keep their
Earth passport. They add the WID. They keep their Earth life.
They add the space.

The space gears us for space faring. Earth has one planet. The
space has the whole sky. The penny teaches value here. The W
Token carries that value off-world.

## Part 3 — Phase-out

The penny taught the lesson: weight, honesty, no fractional
reserve, no printing trust out of nothing (RND-6, RND-7). The
lesson is complete. The old penny is the old penny.

When a person accepts the phase-out, they choose the ceremony.
They bring a penny. The Mint weighs it. The machine stamps a W
into the metal. The penny stops being a penny. It becomes their
W Token.

The stamp happens once. The Mint never stamps a W Token again.
No one mints more W Tokens. The supply is the supply. The cap is
the cap.

## Part 4 — The ceremony

The stamp happens inside the ceremony. The ceremony is a
feeling-special moment. It exists so the person feels special.
That is its whole job. Humans want to feel special. The space
says: yes, you are.

The documentation names it plainly. The ceremony record is filed
in `ceremonies/` as `ceremony-<WID>-<ordinal>.md`. It states: this
was a feeling-special moment, made for this person, so they feel
special. The record is honest about the purpose. Honesty is part
of the gift.

The room applauds. The witnesses give gifts. The gifts go to the
person's start: tools, coins, a place to build, a first contract.
If the person has no parents and no establishment, the witnesses
give them a start by the societal standards of the government.
The standards are in the Registry. The start is a seat on the
Ship (GOV-15), a WID (REG-2), a penny account (GOV-14), and the
road into the crew.

## Part 5 — The W Token is the trust

The W Token is not money. It is proof: this person chose the
space, the space chose them, and the room witnessed both. It is
societal credit. It is a token of humanity. It carries the Coin
Key, the ceremony record, and the chain hash.

The W Token opens doors the penny could not open. It buys a seat
on the Ship. It buys a verified start in the Registry. It buys a
voice in the Vote (GOV-17). It buys a place in the Archive
(GOV-16). The penny taught value on Earth. The W Token carries
the person and their value to the sky.

## Part 6 — The record

Every W Token has a ledger row in `mint/tokens.csv`:

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `token_id` | WT-WID-ordinal |
| `wid` | the citizen's WID |
| `penny_key` | the old penny's Coin Key |
| `stamped` | date of the ceremony |
| `ceremony` | link to the ceremony record |
| `chain_hash` | SHA-256 link into PennyChain |

The chain never forgets. The Archive holds the ceremony records.
The Vote counts the token holders. The Ship seats them.
