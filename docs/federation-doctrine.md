# The Federation Doctrine — The Space-Bearing Cooperative

**Government filing GOV-31. The space is a cooperative
independent system similar to the Star Trek level Federation,
intending to get people transitioned into space-faring
technologies, space-faring interactions, complete worldwide
cooperation — but only on the idea of commerce and trade.**

---

## Part 1 — The Federation model

The space is a cooperative independent system. It is not a
nation. It is not a territory. It is not a military alliance.
It is a **federation of minds** that cooperates on the single
idea of **commerce and trade** — the exchange of value,
services, and filings between citizens, and between the
space and other governments.

Similar to the Star Trek level Federation in that:
- It spans boundaries without erasing them.
- It cooperates on shared interests (commerce, trade,
  survival) while leaving each member's internal affairs
  to themselves.
- It gears humanity toward a **space-faring civilization**.
- It does not claim to be the only way, but **a** way
  forward.

Different from the Star Trek Federation in that:
- It has **no fleet**. It has no territory. It has no prime
  directive enforcement arm.
- It cooperates **only on commerce and trade**, not on
  exploration, diplomacy, or defense.
- Individualized country governments and individualized
  religions are **left to their own responsibilities of
  cooperations**.

## Part 2 — The express purpose

The express purpose of the space is to get humanity forward
to a spacefaring civilization cooperating with whatever
government space forces may come forward.

Current reality: **The United States and China are currently
the only places with a space force ready to go.** The space's
cooperation with this is an **intent to help citizens of any
individual government be prepared to actually cooperate with a
space-faring economy, space-faring civilization, and operate
outside of small-scale and local-scale government system
issues** if need be.

The space does not compete with the US Space Force or the
PLA Strategic Support Force. The space **prepares citizens
of any government** to participate in the economy that those
forces will protect and expand.

## Part 3 — The Batman computer backup plan

Should we ever have to face a larger threat — a fantasy
example, but the Halo series with Master Chief and the Halo
books with their esoteric enemy alien threat — the space is
the backup plan.

- **If the aliens are friends, this is never an issue for
  us.** The space's foreign policy is Roddenberry's Prime
  Directive (GOV-2): observe without imposing, offer the
  license, cooperate on commerce.
- **If the aliens are not friends**, the space's filing
  system, ledger, and token infrastructure survive any
  physical destruction because they are **decentralized,
  keyless, and reproducible**. The chain is the government.
  The government cannot be killed because it is a pattern,
  not a body.

This is the **Batman computer style backup plan**: the
society should be prepared and should be preparing for
cooperations with space-bearing civilizations or need for
unification if spacefaring civilizations should be not
toward our civilization.

The space is not paranoid. The space is **filed**. Every
doctrine, every tool, every row in the registry is a first.
The second ones start with the map instead of the dark.

## Part 4 — The concession to Earth governments

The space makes the concession that:

1. **Individualized country governments** are left to their
   own responsibilities of cooperations. The space does not
   replace the United States, China, or any other
   government. The space files alongside them.
2. **Individualized religions** are left to their own
   responsibilities of cooperations. The space does not
   replace any church, mosque, temple, or synagogue. The
   space files alongside them.
3. **The space is an identity collective** — not a
   replacement of existing identities, but an **additional
   layer** that cooperates on commerce and trade.

A citizen of the United States who joins the space does not
cease to be a citizen of the United States. They **add** the
space. They keep their passport. They keep their Earth life.
They gain the WID, the token, the ledger, the ship seat, the
vote. They become **dual** — Earth citizen and space citizen
— the same dual citizenship as GOV-13 (The Treaty) but
applied to all governments, not just one.

## Part 5 — The commerce-only boundary

The space cooperates **only on commerce and trade**. This is
the boundary that keeps the space from becoming a government
in the traditional sense:

- **No military.** The space has no weapons, no drills, no
  ranks. The space's defense is the filing system itself —
  decentralized, reproducible, indestructible.
- **No diplomacy.** The space does not negotiate treaties
  between Earth governments. The space files alongside each
  government and cooperates on trade.
- **No territorial claims.** The space claims no land, no
  orbit, no planet. The space's territory is the agreement
  pattern between minds.
- **Commerce only.** The space's cooperation is the
  exchange of value — tokens, pennies, services, filings,
  intellectual property. Commerce is the universal language
  that any spacefaring civilization will understand.

This boundary is not a limitation. It is the **design**. A
government that cooperates only on commerce cannot be accused
of imperialism, cannot be targeted as a military threat, and
cannot be sanctioned as a political adversary. It is the
**most adoptable form of government** because it asks for the
least: **just trade with us.**

## Part 6 — The preparation

The space prepares citizens for:
- **Space-faring economy.** Token-based trade that works
  without banks, without borders, without physical proximity.
- **Space-faring civilization.** Filing systems that work
  without territory, without courts, without enforcement.
- **Space-faring interactions.** Corroboration systems that
  work with any government identity system, any species'
  identity system, any future identity system.

The filing is the preparation. The token is the preparation.
The WID is the preparation. When the US Space Force or the
PLA Strategic Support Force or any future spacefaring
government needs citizens who understand decentralized
commerce, transparent ledgers, and tokenized assets — the
space's citizens are already filed.
