# The Progeny Doctrine — Population, Age Gaps, and the Death Loop

**Government filing GOV-32. The space preserves anthropology, not
only history. The age-gap war between young and old creates a
progeny death loop. The space files the solution: a lower-level
economy that allows all to participate, and a constitutional
hardline against the distractions that prevent propagation.**

---

## Part 1 — The problem with history

History is a singular perspective. It records the victors, the
rulers, the visible. It does not record the anthological truth:
the culture, the reproduction, the propagation of the species.

**Anthropology > History** for the space because:
- History asks: who ruled?
- Anthropology asks: how did they live, reproduce, and propagate?

The space preserves **memes** (units of cultural transmission, per
Dawkins 1976, *The Selfish Gene*). A meme that does not reproduce
is a dead meme. A society that does not reproduce is a dead
society. The space files: **anthropology is the preservation of
living culture; history is the filing of dead culture.**

## Part 2 — The progeny death loop

The identity politics of age — young versus old — creates a
progeny death loop:

1. The old want to survive (biological imperative).
2. The old are willing to sacrifice the young to survive
   (resource capture: healthcare, housing, debt).
3. The young, seeing no future, choose not to have children
   (rational economic response).
4. Fewer children → fewer future workers → more pressure on the
   young → further disincentive to reproduce.
5. The loop accelerates until the population collapses.

This is not a theory. This is the current demographic trajectory
of Japan, South Korea, Italy, and increasingly the United States
and China. The total fertility rate in South Korea is 0.80 (2025), down from 0.75 (2024)
children per woman (2024). Replacement is 2.1. The math is
terminal.

## Part 3 — The space's filing

The space does not solve the progeny death loop through
socialism (which has failed to raise birth rates in every
country that tried it). The space solves it through:

### 3.1 — A lower-level economy
Every Roblox game has a bottom-level item that new players can
earn. When you are homeless, you cannot earn the bottom-level
item. The space's economy creates the bottom-level entry point:
- A basic task system (not UBI — earned, not given).
- Education that connects to jobs.
- Jobs that connect to the extended economy.
- The extended economy that connects to the space's trade.

The lower-level economy is not charity. It is **the on-ramp**.
It meets people where they are, not where they "should be."

### 3.2 — Anti-socialism, pro-capitalism-with-concession
The space **denounces socialism** in all its forms:
- Socialism concentrates power (the state decides).
- Socialism creates dependency (the state provides).
- Socialism kills reproduction (no incentive to propagate
  when the state raises your children).

The space **embraces capitalism-with-concession**:
- Capitalism creates incentive (you keep what you earn).
- Capitalism creates abundance (the rising tide).
- Capitalism requires a floor (without a floor, capitalism
  devours itself).

The concession is **concessionary funds that operate as
operational ledgers**. The space does not give money. The
space creates tasks, jobs, and economic surfaces that allow
people to earn. The ledger is public. The accounting is
transparent. The "concession" is that the space accepts
anyone at the bottom level — but they must climb from there.

### 3.3 — The anthropological mission
The space preserves anthropology:
- Record how people live, not just who ruled.
- File the culture, the reproduction, the propagation.
- Ensure the memes survive (the culture, the rituals, the
  family structures that produce children).

The Timeline (GOV-22/23/24/25/27) tracks civilizations. The
anthropological filing tracks **how they lived and reproduced**.
A civilization that stopped reproducing is a dead
civilization, regardless of how magnificent its ruins.

## Part 4 — The hard solution

The space files plainly: **the progeny death loop is not solved
by argument. It is solved by creating a system where young people
can afford to have children.**

This requires:
- Housing that young people can afford (the Land Question,
  ECO-4: Georgism, LVT, vacant lot filing).
- Education that connects to employment (the lower-level
  economy).
- A culture that values propagation (the anthropological
- filing).
- An economy that does not punish parenthood (the concessionary
  ledger).

The space is that system. The space is not a government that
talks about the problem. The space is a government that creates
the ledger, the tasks, the jobs, the housing, and the culture
that allows the young to propagate.
