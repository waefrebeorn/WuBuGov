# The Education Efficacy Doctrine — Does the Government Teach?

**Government filing GOV-26. The First Ones claim (GOV-25):
"we are taught as we grow; teaching is the community's work."
Education efficacy measures that claim. Every filing climbs
four rungs. TEACH means readable (RND-20). PRACTICE means the
filing ships a tool (GOV-10). PROOF means the tool has proof.
CHAIN means the registry resolves it.**

---

## Part 1 — The four rungs

A filing teaches when a newcomer can do four things:

1. **TEACH** — read the doctrine. The file passes the
   plain-English gate (FRES CLEAR, RND-20).
2. **PRACTICE** — run its tool. Every doctrine ships a real
   tool (GOV-10: no doctrine without a tool).
3. **PROOF** — watch the tool prove itself. The tool has a
   test or a documented verification.
4. **CHAIN** — find the filing in the map of the whole. The
   registry resolves it; no phantom references.

A filing that climbs all four rungs teaches. A filing that
climbs three teaches with a hole in the stair. The rungs are
not decorations; they are the stairs a newcomer climbs from
reading to doing.

## Part 2 — The measure

`tools/wubu_edu.py` scores every filed doctrine on the four
rungs and renders EDUCATION_EFFICACY.md. The verdict is one
number: the share of filings that teach on all four rungs.

The tool is the report card. It does not grade on effort; it
grades on the climb. A doctrine that reads well but ships no
tool teaches words, not work. A tool with no proof teaches
confidence, not truth. The rungs keep the grade honest.

## Part 3 — The first verdict (filed with this doctrine)

The first run of the tool found the honest score. **23 of 62
filings teach on all four rungs.** The CHAIN rung broke first:
the audit caught RND-26 referenced but unregistered. With the
registration fixed, the chain verifies. The TEACH rung broke
wider: the Ledger's nine doctrines all fail the plain-English
gate, so none of them teach on all four rungs.

The tool earned its keep in the same breath that built it. It
found the phantom reference it depended on. It found that the
Ledger — the division that moves value — is the division that
teaches least. Both are now filed as findings, not
decorations.

## Part 4 — The lesson

Education efficacy is the government's own report card, and
the first card is honest. A government that claims to teach
must measure its teaching. The measure is public; the gaps
are public; the next filing closes them.

The First Ones said: we are taught as we grow. The rungs are
the growth. The measure is the file. The climb is the
community's work.
