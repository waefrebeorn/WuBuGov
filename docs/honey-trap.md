# The Honey Trap — Gamified Fast Track for the Masses

**Government filing GOV-42 (replaces GOV-41). The masses will not
flock to a fire. They need honey. The Fast Track is honey: sweet,
sticky, and full of bees. The ignorant rich buy in. The smart
poor earn in. Everyone plays. The game is the filter.**

---

## Part 1 — The game is the honey

The original Fast Track (GOV-41) was a Dwight Schrute spin circle.
That is fire. Nobody walks into fire for fun.

The Honey Trap is different. The Honey Trap is:
- **Sweet** — you get visible rewards immediately.
- **Sticky** — the more you play, the more you want to play.
- **Full of bees** — if you try to cheat the game, you get stung.

The game is called: **WUBU WORM**

## Part 2 — WUBU WORM: the game

You are a worm. You eat plastic. You grow. You climb. You win.

### 2.1 — The worm

Every citizen gets a **worm** — a small, visible avatar on the
ledger. The worm starts as a tiny dot. The worm grows by
eating **plastic tokens**.

- **Tiny worm** — level 0. Just joined. No tokens.
- **Small worm** — level 1. First plastic token eaten.
- **Medium worm** — level 5. Five tokens. Growing.
- **Large worm** — level 10. Ten tokens. Respectable.
- **Huge worm** — level 25. Twenty-five tokens. Impressive.
- **Mega worm** — level 50. Fifty tokens. A leader.
- **Ultra worm** — level 100. One hundred tokens. Legendary.
- **Cosmic worm** — level 250. Two hundred fifty tokens. Mythic.

The worm is **visible on the public ledger**. Everyone sees
your worm. Everyone sees your level. The worm is your status.
The worm is your identity. The worm is the honey.

### 2.2 — Eating plastic

You grow your worm by eating plastic tokens. Plastic tokens
are earned by:

- **Doing tasks** — filing research, verifying tokens, adding
  plastic to the collection.
- **Buying Fast Track** — paying money for instant plastic
  tokens (the ignorant rich path).
- **Cooperating** — other citizens gift you plastic tokens
  for helpful contributions.
- **Discovering** — finding plastic tokens on the street
  (lost tokens are re-minted to the finder).

Each plastic token eaten = 1 worm point. Worm points = level.
Level = status. Status = honey.

### 2.3 — The climb

The worm climbs a **visible ladder** on the ledger:

```
Level 250 ── COSMIC WORM ── █████████████████████████
Level 100 ── ULTRA WORM   ─────────────────────────
Level  50 ── MEGA WORM    ─────────────────────────
Level  25 ── HUGE WORM    ─────────────────────────
Level  10 ── LARGE WORM   ─────────────────────────
Level   5 ── MEDIUM WORM  ─────────────────────────
Level   1 ── SMALL WORM   ─────────────────────────
Level   0 ── TINY WORM    ───────────── YOU ARE HERE
```

Everyone sees the ladder. Everyone sees where you are.
Everyone sees where everyone else is. The ladder is the
honey. The ladder is the motivation. The ladder is the game.

## Part 3 — The Fast Track: pay to grow

The Fast Track lets you **buy growth**:

| Purchase | Cost (pennies) | Worm Points | Level Skip |
|----------|----------------|-------------|------------|
| Snack | 100 | +1 | Tiny → Small |
| Meal | 500 | +5 | Small → Medium |
| Feast | 2,500 | +25 | Medium → Huge |
| Banquet | 10,000 | +50 | Huge → Mega |
| Coronation | 50,000 | +100 | Mega → Ultra |
| Apotheosis | 250,000 | +250 | Ultra → Cosmic |

The Fast Track is **instant**. Pay the money, get the points,
grow the worm immediately. The ignorant rich see this and
think: *I can buy a cosmic worm.*

They can. But the cosmic worm does nothing by itself.

## Part 4 — The bees (the sting)

The worm has **powers** that are unlocked by cooperation,
not by payment:

| Power | Requirement | What it does |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **Vote** | 50 tokens + 10 cooperation acts | Cast a vote in the space |
| **Ship Seat** | 100 tokens + 25 cooperation acts | Reserve a seat on the Ship |
| **Bureau Access** | 250 tokens + 50 cooperation acts | View Bureau reports |
| **Policy Voice** | 500 tokens + 100 cooperation acts | Propose new filings |
| **Founder Ear** | 1,000 tokens + 250 cooperation acts | Direct message to founder |

**The sting:** You can buy your way to a cosmic worm. But a
cosmic worm with no cooperation acts has **no powers**. It is
a big, visible, powerless worm. It sits at the top of the
ladder and does nothing.

The ignorant rich who bought their way to cosmic worm see:
- Their worm is huge.
- Their worm has no powers.
- Other worms are smaller but have powers.
- The smaller worms are climbing faster (because cooperation
  earns bonus points).

**The bee sting:** The ledger shows:
```
COSMIC WORM (Level 250) — NO POWERS — 0 cooperation acts
ULTRA WORM (Level 100) — VOTE, SHIP SEAT — 35 cooperation acts
MEGA WORM (Level 50) — VOTE, SHIP SEAT, BUREAU — 75 cooperation acts
```

The ignorant rich see this. Some of them learn: *cooperation
is the real power.* Some of them get angry: *I paid all this
money for a powerless worm!* Some of them leave.

The ones who learn become real citizens. The ones who leave
funded the base economy. The game filters.

## Part 5 — The cooperation multiplier

Cooperation earns **bonus points**:

| Action | Base Points | Cooperation Multiplier | Total |
|--------|-------------|------------------------|-------|
| File research | +1 | x2 if verified | +2 |
| Verify token | +1 | x3 if first verify | +3 |
| Add plastic | +1 | x2 if sorted | +2 |
| Gift token | +2 | x2 if to newbie | +4 |
| Cooperate | +5 | x2 if cross-level | +10 |

The smart poor earn **faster** than the rich. The rich pay
for 25 points. The smart poor earn 25 points in a week of
cooperation. The smart poor's worm has powers. The rich
worm does not.

This is the honey: **everyone can play, but cooperation
wins.**

## Part 6 — The daily login bonus

The worm gets hungry. You must **log in daily** to feed it:

- **Daily login** = +1 point (just show up).
- **Daily task** = +5 points (do one small thing).
- **Daily cooperation** = +10 points (help another citizen).

If you don't log in for 7 days, your worm **shrinks** by 1
point. The worm is alive. The worm needs you. The worm is
the honey that brings you back.

## Part 7 — The worm fights

Worms can **fight** each other — but only through cooperation:

- **Challenge:** Another worm's level is higher. You challenge
  them to a cooperation duel.
- **Duel:** Both worms perform cooperation tasks for 24 hours.
  Most tasks completed = winner.
- **Reward:** Winner gets +10 points. Loser loses nothing
  (this is not a punishment game — this is a motivation game).
- **Spectacle:** The duel is public. Everyone watches. The
  worms become famous. Fame = honey.

The ignorant rich cannot buy their way out of duels. Duels
are cooperation only. The rich either learn to cooperate or
lose publicly.

## Part 8 — The worm marketplace

Worms can **trade** plastic tokens:

- **Buy tokens** from the space (Fast Track — the ignorant
  rich path).
- **Sell tokens** to other citizens (earn their pennies).
- **Gift tokens** to newbies (earn cooperation multiplier).
- **Burn tokens** for special rewards (rare items, unique
  worm skins, ledger badges).

The marketplace is the honey. The marketplace is alive. The
marketplace is where the game happens.

## Part 9 — The worm skins

Worms can have **skins** (visual customizations):

| Skin | How to get | Rarity |
|------|------------|--------|
| Default | Free | Common |
| Copper | 50 tokens | Uncommon |
| Silver | 100 tokens + 50 cooperation | Rare |
| Gold | 500 tokens + 100 cooperation | Epic |
| Diamond | 1,000 tokens + 250 cooperation | Legendary |
| Cosmic | Founder award | Mythic |

The skins are **visible on the ledger**. The skins are
status. The skins are the honey. The skins cannot be bought
with money alone. The skins require cooperation.

## Part 10 — The worm halls of fame

The ledger tracks:

- **Longest worm** (highest level).
- **Fastest climb** (most points in 30 days).
- **Most cooperative** (most cooperation acts).
- **Best trader** (most tokens traded).
- **Best duelist** (most duels won).
- **Best mentor** (most newbies helped).

The halls of fame are **permanent**. The halls of fame are
**on the chain**. The halls of fame are the honey. Everyone
wants to be in the halls of fame. The halls of fame require
cooperation.
