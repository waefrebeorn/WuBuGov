## Summary of Systemic Risks

1. **Founder Single Point of Failure**: The entire system depends on the founder for funding (GOV-65), veto power (GOV-64), succession trigger (GOV-64), and constitutional interpretation (GOV-34). No multi-sig or distributed authority exists today.

2. **Enforcement Gap**: The space claims powerlessness (GOV-1) but also claims enforcement capability (GOV-47). These cannot both be true simultaneously. The Penny Clause is acknowledged as unenforceable for trivial amounts (GOV-74), making it symbolic rather than legal.

3. **Token Definition Chaos**: The W Token is defined in at least 3 incompatible ways across GOV-19, GOV-28, and GOV-30. Citizens cannot know what they hold — is it an identity anchor, a social score, or a ceremonial artifact?

4. **Succession Fragility**: Only one backup (Bureau Chief) exists before the World Congress must elect a new founder. If both founder and Bureau Chief are incapacitated simultaneously, the government has no clear continuity.

5. **Legal Jurisdiction Void**: GOV-64 says the space is "governed by the license" but the license is an open-source copyright license, not a governing law. No court would recognize the license as a constitution. The space has no legal personality (no corporation, no trust, no foundation).
