## Part 1 — The research, filed with sources

### 1.1 The OECD Digital Government Policy Framework (2020)

The OECD defines a fully digital government by **six dimensions**
(OECD 2020, DOI 10.1787/f64fed2a-en). Four are base. Two change how
the rest work.

**Base:**

1. **Digital by design.** Digital is embedded in the policy cycle.
   It is not bolted on. "Digital means simple and collaborative."
2. **Data-driven public sector.** Data is a base enabler. Data rules
   underpin trusted services.
3. **Government as a platform.** One shared base that services build
   on. Not separate silos per department.
4. **Open by default.** Open data, open records, and open doors are
   the default. They are not exceptions.

**Change makers:**

5. **User-driven.** Services are built around people's needs. Not the
   office's ease. "Governments do not design public services to serve
   an organisation... but as a response to people's needs."
6. **Proactiveness.** The government sees what comes. It acts before
   it is asked. (Estonia's X-Road is the model for the platform
   dimension: once-only data, zero trust, no silos.)

The OECD's key line, filed: a mature digital government delivers
"public services that match and potentially exceed people's
expectations." Public services are "the most immediate experience
people have of their governments."

### 1.2 The OECD rule on Open Government (2017)

The OECD rule (OECD/LEGAL-0438) makes open government a shift in how
power works. It rests on **four principles**:

- **Transparency** — the public sees what government does
- **Integrity** — the public can trust what it sees
- **Accountability** — the public can call it to account
- **Stakeholder participation** — the public helps shape it

### 1.3 The three levels of citizen involvement (OECD 2022)

The OECD defines three levels of citizen involvement (2022, DOI
10.1787/f765caf6-en). Each is a real tie. Not a slogan.

1. **Information** — one-way. The government makes and sends the
   info. Both on demand and on its own.
2. **Consultation** — two-way. Citizens give views. The government
   answers. It starts with a clear question. It reports back on what
   it did.
3. **Engagement** — side by side. Citizens get the tools (data,
   time, software) to work with the government through the whole
   cycle. They have **equal standing** in setting the agenda. The
   final call often still rests with the authority.

The OECD's counter-myth, filed: citizens who "only show up to complain"
are a symptom of a process built to collect complaints. A process
that gathers ideas, co-builds answers, or co-runs programs gets real
work from citizens. The OECD says it plainly: "they will work in a
constructive, meaningful way."

### 1.4 The language layer (cross-file RND-20)

A government that is transparent but unreadable is still opaque. The
Language Doctrine (RND-20) is the writing half of this doctrine. Every
public filing must measure CLEAR (FRES ≥ 60, grade ≤ 8) on the space's
`plain_lang` checker. Digital by design without plain by default is a
faster way to confuse people.

## Part 2 — The doctrine, enacted

### Article I — The six dimensions, mapped to the space

1. **Digital by design.** Every institution of the space ships as a
   repo, a form, and a C11 tool. Never as a promise. The WuBuCore
   suite (WID, Penny Score, PennyChain, plain_lang) is the proof. The
   space's laws run as code. The code is public.
2. **Data-driven.** The ledgers are public and append-only: the
   Bureau's reports.csv, actions.csv, penny-audit.csv, the PennyChain
   Merkle forest. The space's data is a first-class citizen. It is
   keyless, verifiable, and every tamper attempt changes the root.
3. **Government as a platform.** WuBuCore is the platform. One shared
   C11 core. Every module self-contained and reusing the same verified
   math. The repos are the services on top. No silos: the WID from
   WuBuCitizen is the same WID the Penny Score uses. It is the same
   WID the PennyChain records.
4. **Open by default.** Everything is public except the vault (the 7
   Magic Keys). And the vault's existence is public. The Bureau
   watches the Bureau. There are no black sites. Open by default means
   the license (§0 Anyone May Use) is the default for the space's
   work.
5. **User-driven.** The user is the first one. The citizen holding the
   penny. Services are written for that reader (RND-20). They are
   priced for that citizen (the Penny Clause: physical pennies). They
   are designed around the citizen's filing, not the institution's
   convenience.
6. **Proactiveness.** The space anticipates. The Bureau files before
   the threat lands (see-something-say-something). The economics
   division files the transition before the crash (the four
   inflations, the lifespan loop). The tools flag their own violations
   (plain_lang, mirror_score). A proactive space protects its people.
   It does not wait to be asked.

### Article II — The participation ladder (enacted)

The space adopts the OECD three levels as its ladder of citizenship.

1. **Information (one-way).** Every filing is published. Every ledger
   is public. Every score is explained. The citizen does not need to
   ask.
2. **Consultation (two-way).** Forms accept amendments. The Greed
   Index's ladder invites the flagged actor to file (the off-ramp).
   WBI-1 reports are answered. Results are published. A consultation
   that answers nothing is a mirror. And mirrors are prohibited
   (GOV-3, RND-18).
3. **Engagement (side by side).** The next institutions on the filing
   order — The Vote, The Assembly, The Archive — are built for this
   rung. Citizens get equal standing to set the agenda. They get the
   data and tools to work with the space. A first one who files a
   better law than the Bureau's is not a nuisance. They are the
   point.

### Article III — The four principles, enacted

- **Transparency:** every action of the space is a filing. Every
  filing is public. The ledger screams.
- **Integrity:** the math is published (WID weights, Penny Score v2
  weights, the Flesch-Kincaid formulas in RND-20). "Transparent
  scoring" means the citizen can recompute their own score by hand.
- **Accountability:** the Bureau watches the Bureau. The messenger is
  sacred. Forms end with a name, a date, and a check digit.
- **Participation:** the ladder of Article II is a right. Not a
  courtesy.

### Article IV — The plain-language floor (cross-file RND-20)

No filing of the space is published above grade 8. The checker
(`bin/plain_lang`, WuBuCore) is the gate. Exit 0 means CLEAR. Exit 1
means rewrite. This doctrine and every doctrine it amends are
themselves filed through that gate.
