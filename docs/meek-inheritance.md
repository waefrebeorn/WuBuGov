# The Meek Inheritance — Removing Barriers, Creating Purpose

**Government filing GOV-56. The meek and mild shall inherit the
earth. The wasteful shall waste away. Research verified: barriers
to entry prevent ordinary people from participating (Iowa State
Extension, Investopedia). Universal Basic Income provides security
but not purpose (Joseph Rowntree Foundation, UNC). Microtask
platforms provide work but often without dignity (MTurk). The
~ WuBu ~ space must do all three: remove barriers, provide
purpose, and preserve dignity.**

---

## Part 1 — The Problem with Meta-Gaming

### 1.1 — What is Meta-Gaming?

Meta-gaming is when the smart and the loud optimize the system
for their own benefit. They:
- Read the fine print to find loopholes.
- Pay for Fast Track to skip the base.
- Buy decorative tokens without cooperating.
- Speak loudly in the Vote without filing.
- Take credit for others' work.

Meta-gaming is not illegal. Meta-gaming is not immoral. But
meta-gaming undermines the purpose of the space.

### 1.2 — The Meek and Mild

The meek and mild are:
- The ones who do not read the fine print.
- The ones who do not have money for Fast Track.
- The ones who are too quiet to speak in the Vote.
- The ones who are too busy surviving to file research.
- The ones who are mentally unwell, physically disabled, or
  just plain tired.

The meek and mild are the ones the space was built for.

### 1.3 — The Wasteful

The wasteful are:
- The ones who pay for Fast Track and think they have arrived.
- The ones who buy decorative tokens and display them without
  understanding.
- The ones who speak in the Vote without filing research.
- The ones who optimize for personal gain without contributing
  to the collective.

The wasteful are not punished. The wasteful waste away naturally.

## Part 2 — Removing Barriers

### 2.1 — The Five Barriers

Research identifies five barriers to entry:
1. **Knowledge barriers** — you must know the system exists.
2. **Complexity barriers** — you must understand the system.
3. **Resource barriers** — you must have money or time.
4. **Social barriers** — you must know people in the system.
5. **Dignity barriers** — you must not feel like a beggar.

### 2.2 — Removing Each Barrier

| Barrier | Solution | Implementation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| **Knowledge** | Make the space findable | Simple website, word of mouth, lodge flyers |
| **Complexity** | Make the system readable | One-page onboarding, visual guides, lodge mentors |
| **Resources** | Make it free to start | 1 penny to join, lodge mentors help with pennies |
| **Social** | Make it welcoming | Lodge system, mentors, no cliques |
| **Dignity** | Make it meaningful | Earned tokens, visible progress, purpose-driven missions |

### 2.3 — The One-Penny Promise

The space promises: **one penny is enough.**

You do not need money. You do not need crypto. You do not need
a bank account. You need one physical penny.

If you do not have a penny:
- A lodge mentor will give you one.
- A citizen will gift you one.
- The W Mint will give you one (no questions asked).

The one-penny promise is sacred. No citizen is turned away for
lack of a penny.

## Part 3 — The Meek Inheritance

### 3.1 — What the Meek Inherit

The meek inherit:
- **A WID** — permanent, theirs forever.
- **A worm** — visible, growing, theirs to feed.
- **A voice** — one WID, one vote (weighted by cooperation,
  not money).
- **A lodge** — a physical place to belong.
- **A mission** — purpose-driven work that matters.
- **A share** — of the collective abundance.

### 3.2 — How the Meek Inherit

The meek inherit by:
1. **Showing up** — logging in, being present.
2. **Doing small things** — verifying a token, adding plastic,
   attending a lodge meeting.
3. **Cooperating** — gifting tokens, helping others, teaching.
4. **Staying** — not leaving when it gets hard.
5. **Growing** — climbing the worm, choosing a branch.

The meek do not inherit by paying. The meek do not inherit by
gaming. The meek inherit by being.

### 3.3 — The Natural Consequence

The wasteful waste away because:
- Decorative tokens have no powers.
- Fast Track does not grant governance.
- Loud voices without filing are not counted.
- Money does not buy cooperation.
- The system rewards the meek and ignores the wasteful.

This is not punishment. This is natural consequence. The wasteful
waste away because they waste away. The meek inherit because they
inherit.

## Part 4 — The Dignity-First Microtask System

### 4.1 — The Problem with Microtasks

Research shows: microtask platforms (MTurk) connect workers with
small tasks. But the work is often:
- Boring (data entry, image labeling).
- Underpaid (cents per task).
- Invisible (no credit, no meaning).
- Exploitative (workers are disposable).

The ~ WuBu ~ space rejects this model.

### 4.2 — The ~ WuBu ~ Microtask Model

The ~ WuBu ~ microtask model is:
- **Meaningful** — every task has a visible purpose.
- **Dignified** — every task is credited, every worker is named.
- **Fair** — every task pays a fair token wage (not cents).
- **Social** — every task connects workers to each other.
- **Progressive** — every task builds towards something bigger.

### 4.3 — The Task Types

| Task Type | Description | Token Wage | Skill Required |
|-----------|-------------|------------|----------------|
| **Verify** | Verify a token or filing | 1-5 WT | None |
| **Sort** | Sort plastic, classify coins | 1-3 WT | None |
| **File** | File a research page | 10 WT | Low |
| **Mentor** | Teach a new citizen | 30 WT | Medium |
| **Audit** | Audit a filing for accuracy | 50 WT | Medium |
| **Build** | Build a tool or app | 100-500 WT | High |
| **Lead** | Lead a lodge or mission | 100-1000 WT | High |

### 4.4 — The Task Principles

1. **No task is too small.** Sorting plastic is as valuable as
   filing research. Both are needed. Both are credited.

2. **No task is invisible.** Every completed task is logged on
   the chain. Every worker is named. Every contribution is
   visible.

3. **No task is disposable.** Workers are not disposable. Every
   worker is a citizen. Every citizen has a WID. Every WID has
   a voice.

4. **No task is the end.** Every task builds towards something.
   Sorting plastic → stamping coins → minting tokens → funding
   the base economy → helping the poor. The chain is visible.

## Part 5 — The Anti-Meta-Gaming Rules

### 5.1 — The Five Rules

1. **No Pay-to-Win.** Money buys tokens, not powers. Powers
   come from cooperation.

2. **No Loud Voices.** The Vote is weighted by cooperation
   score, not by how loud you speak.

3. **No Invisible Work.** Every task is logged. Every worker
   is named. No free-riding.

4. **No Permanent Hierarchy.** Titles (Mangi, Sultan, Emperor)
   are earned by cooperation, not bought. They decay if you
   stop cooperating.

5. **No Exclusion.** The meek and mild are always welcome. The
   wasteful are not punished — they simply waste away.

### 5.2 — The Decay System

Powers decay:
- If you do not cooperate for 30 days, your cooperation score
  drops.
- If your cooperation score drops, your powers unlock.
- If your powers unlock, you must re-earn them.
- Re-earning is easy — just cooperate again.

This is not punishment. This is natural consequence. Powers are
not owned. Powers are earned.

### 5.3 — The Visibility System

Everything is visible:
- Every filing is on the chain.
- Every token is on the chain.
- Every mission is on the chain.
- Every penny is on the chain.
- Every citizen is on the chain.

If you game the system, everyone sees. If you cooperate,
everyone sees. The chain is the judge.

## Part 7 — The Fulfillment Loop

### 7.1 — The Loop

```
Purpose → Mission → Action → Reward → Growth → New Purpose → Repeat
```

The loop is not a hamster wheel. The loop is a spiral. Each
loop takes the citizen higher. Each loop gives the citizen
more purpose. Each loop makes the citizen more themselves.

### 7.2 — The Three Fulfillments

| Need | How the Space Fulfills |
|------|------------------------|
| **Autonomy** | The citizen chooses their own missions. No forced tasks. |
| **Competence** | The citizen grows their worm, earns powers, sees progress. |
| **Relatedness** | The citizen belongs to a lodge, cooperates with others. |

### 7.3 — The Fulfillment Metrics

The ledger tracks:
- **Missions completed** — by type, branch, difficulty.
- **Fulfillment score** — autonomy + competence + relatedness.
- **Branch progress** — how far the citizen has climbed.
- **Lodge contribution** — how much the citizen has given.
